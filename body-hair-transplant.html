What Is Body Hair Transplant

A specialized procedure to restore or enhance body hair in multiple areas

Restore Body Hair Naturally

Body hair transplant is a specialized FUE procedure that restores or enhances hair growth on areas where hair is thin, patchy, or absent. Using advanced microneedle technology, healthy follicles are carefully harvested from donor areas and strategically implanted to create natural, permanent results.

Whether you want a fuller chest, enhanced beard line, restored pubic hair, or coverage for scars, our surgeons understand the unique growth patterns of each body area and tailor their technique accordingly.

Our Body Hair Transplant Technique

Advanced FUE microneedle technology for natural, permanent results

1

Consultation & Design

We discuss your goals and examine the treatment area. Our surgeon designs a customized hair pattern that matches your body type and aesthetic preferences. We mark the exact placement areas and discuss expected density.

2

Donor Hair Selection

We carefully select the best donor hair for your body area. For chest and beard, we often use beard hair because it matches the thickness and texture. For other areas, we may use scalp hair or body hair from other regions.

3

Graft Extraction

Using our 0.6mm microneedle FUE system, we extract individual follicles with minimal scarring. The tiny extraction sites heal quickly and are barely visible, allowing you to keep donor areas short if desired.

4

Graft Preparation

Extracted follicles are immediately placed in a specialized preservation solution and sorted under a microscope. We prepare each graft to maximize survival rate and ensure optimal growth direction.

5

Implantation

Using precision implanter pens, we place each graft at the correct angle and depth to match natural body hair growth patterns. We create natural gradients — denser in the center, lighter at the edges — for authentic-looking results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about body hair restoration

Which body areas are commonly treated with body hair transplant?
The most commonly requested areas include chest hair, abdominal or happy-trail hair, leg hair, pubic hair, and underarm hair. We also regularly treat arm hair, periareolar chest hair around the nipple line, and scar coverage on any body region. During your consultation we review your goals, skin tone, and existing hair patterns to recommend the areas that will deliver the most natural-looking result.
Which donor regions are typically used for body hair grafts?
Our surgeons draw from a carefully selected mix of donor zones depending on the target area. For beard and chest restoration we often prefer beard hair harvested below the jawline because its thickness and coarseness match well. Scalp hair from the safe donor zone works well for finer body regions. For matching leg or underarm texture, we may extract from leg hair, underarm hair, or even pubic hair when appropriate — each follicle keeps its original texture wherever it is transplanted.
How many times can a single body hair follicle be reused?
Each body hair follicle can only be harvested and transplanted one time — once extracted from its donor location it cannot be returned or re-extracted. This is why careful donor management is critical. Our surgeons map your total available donor reserve during consultation and plan conservatively, preserving healthy follicles for future sessions if needed. We never over-harvest any single donor zone.
Does transplanted body hair have a different growth cycle than regular hair?
Transplanted hair keeps the growth cycle characteristics of its original donor site, not the recipient site. For example, beard donor follicles transplanted to your chest will cycle like beard hair, which means faster growth and coarser texture than native chest hair. Scalp donor hair grows longer and cycles more slowly. We blend multiple donor types to recreate the natural mix you would normally see. Full cycling typically stabilizes by month 12 after your procedure.
What density range works best for body hair transplantation?
Unlike scalp transplants that aim for 60-80 grafts per cm², body hair requires lower density for a believable result. Most body areas look natural at 25-45 grafts per cm², with intentional gradients — denser in the center of the chest or happy trail and feathering lighter toward the edges. Over-packing body sites is the most common reason for unnatural, pluggy results. Our surgeons are trained specifically in body-hair density calibration.
Can I receive body hair donated by another person?
No, allogeneic body hair transplantation from another person is not a viable option. Your immune system would reject foreign follicles even with immunosuppressants, and the long-term health risks far outweigh any cosmetic benefit. Every graft we transplant must come from your own donor reserves. For patients with extremely limited donor hair we recommend combining transplant with scalp micropigmentation for the fullest appearance.
Is there any special long-term care required after body hair transplant?
Long-term care is surprisingly minimal. For the first 10 days you keep the recipient sites clean with our provided sterile wash and avoid direct pressure, friction, or tight clothing over the area. After the grafts fully anchor at around two weeks you can resume shaving, trimming, waxing, and exfoliating as normal. Sun protection for the first 3 months helps prevent pigment changes. Beyond that you simply groom your new body hair the same way you would treat native hair.
Will other people notice I had a body hair transplant at the pool or gym showers?
When performed with proper density gradients and direction matching, the results are indistinguishable from natural body hair in shared settings like swimming pools, locker rooms, saunas, and public showers. The tiny 0.6mm extraction sites heal as pinhead dots that fade within weeks, and even closely shaved donor areas appear completely normal. We advise waiting about 4 weeks before public swimming to ensure the skin barrier is fully healed, after which no one can tell the difference.
