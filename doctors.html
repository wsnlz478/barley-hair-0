Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about choosing and working with our surgical team

How should I choose the right Barley doctor for my specific case
Start by reviewing each doctor's listed expertise tags on this page and matching them against your goals. If you want a soft, natural female hairline, prioritize a doctor tagged for hairline design and female cases. If you're covering a large bald area, look for large-area specialization. Your international coordinator will also shortlist two to three surgeons whose case histories most closely match your hair type, loss pattern, and aesthetic expectations before you commit.
What core qualifications and credentials should I verify before choosing a hair transplant surgeon
At minimum, confirm an active medical license with the national health authority, board certification in a surgical or dermatological specialty, documented years of exclusive hair restoration practice rather than occasional cosmetic work, verifiable before-and-after portfolios, and membership in professional organizations like the Asian Hair Restoration Association. All Barley surgeons meet these criteria and their credentials are posted publicly for full transparency.
Can I specifically request to work with a certain doctor, and is there an extra fee for doing so
Yes, you can absolutely request any surgeon from our directory, and there is never a surcharge for choosing a specific physician. During booking, simply tell your coordinator which doctor you prefer. The only limitation is scheduling — our most senior doctors fill up four to six weeks in advance, so international patients are encouraged to lock in their preferred surgeon early when planning their trip.
Approximately how many hair transplant procedures does a Barley surgeon perform each year
Our full-time surgeons perform between 220 and 350 procedures annually, depending on case complexity. Senior doctors with 15+ years of experience typically handle 280 to 350 cases per year, while newer surgeons who graduated from our apprenticeship program start at 220 and grow with mentorship. This volume is intentionally capped to protect quality — we never overbook or rush cases to hit targets.
Do you have female surgeons available, and are there specific advantages to choosing a female doctor
Yes. Several women practice across our clinic network, including Dr. Feng Yuping, Dr. Cui Jinxiu, Dr. Liu Cuiyun, and Dr. Fang Yifang. Many female patients, and some male patients, prefer working with a female surgeon for comfort during the physical exam or for a second opinion on aesthetic decisions. Female doctors at Barley have exactly the same training, credentials, and surgical privileges as their male colleagues.
Are the doctors at your regional branches trained to the same standard as the Beijing and Shanghai headquarters teams
Yes. Every surgeon across all 31 branches completes the same 12 to 18-month Barley certification apprenticeship in Beijing before being cleared to operate independently. All doctors return to headquarters every quarter for mandatory technique refreshers and review sessions with our medical board. Protocols, equipment, audit standards, and graft survival guarantees are identical across every location in the network.
Will my assigned doctor actually perform the critical surgical steps, or do technicians do most of the work
Your assigned lead surgeon personally handles the design, recipient site creation, frontal hairline implantation, and immediate post-operative check. Trained technicians assist with follicle extraction from the donor area and graft sorting under the surgeon's continuous direct supervision. This division maximizes graft survival time without sacrificing quality control on the aesthetically critical portions of your procedure.
How involved is the surgeon in my post-operative care after I fly back home
Your operating surgeon personally reviews every checkpoint photo you submit at day three, week one, month one, three, six, and twelve. When something looks unusual or you have a question, the surgeon responds directly on the video call or via WhatsApp voice note rather than delegating to a coordinator. You also keep direct access to them for any urgent concern throughout your entire first year of recovery.
