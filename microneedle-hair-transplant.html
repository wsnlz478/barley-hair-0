How It Works

Microneedle Hair Transplant Process

Understanding your journey from consultation to final results

1. Consultation & Design

Meet with our specialists to discuss your goals, assess donor area quality, and design your ideal hairline or treatment area. We use advanced imaging technology to preview expected results.

2. Preparation & Anesthesia

The donor area (typically back of scalp) is trimmed and prepared. Local anesthesia is administered for complete comfort throughout the procedure.

3. Follicle Extraction

Individual hair follicles are carefully extracted using precision microneedle tools. Our team selects the healthiest follicles with minimal trauma to ensure optimal survival.

4. Recipient Site Creation

Using ultra-fine microneedles, we create tiny incisions at precise angles and directions matching your natural hair growth pattern. This step is crucial for achieving natural-looking results.

5. Graft Implantation

Extracted follicles are carefully implanted into the prepared sites. Our team works meticulously to ensure proper depth, angle, and distribution for optimal growth and aesthetics.

6. Recovery & Results

The procedure is complete! Most patients return to normal activities within 2-3 days. Transplanted hair will shed initially (normal), with new growth beginning around 3-4 months and final results visible at 12 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our patients ask most often

How is Barley's microneedle service priced differently from regular microneedle clinics?
Barley's proprietary microneedle service is priced per graft, with the premium reflecting our 14 patented needle technologies, the 0.6mm ultra-fine extraction tip, and the dedicated implant pen used throughout placement. While the per-graft rate sits slightly above basic FUE, patients typically need fewer touch-up sessions due to the higher 95%+ survival rate, making the overall journey comparable or even lower in cost. We publish transparent pricing during consultation with no hidden add-ons.
Does every Barley patient receive the implant pen, or is it only for certain cases?
Every microneedle procedure at Barley uses our patented implant pen regardless of case size. The pen allows our surgical team to place each follicle at a precise depth and angle while exerting minimal lateral pressure on the surrounding native hairs. This consistency is one reason why we can achieve such uniform density and natural direction across hairlines, crowns, and density fill areas. Even smaller 800-graft temple touch-ups receive the exact same pen-based implantation standard.
What exactly comes inside the postoperative care kit you provide?
Every patient leaves with a comprehensive kit containing: a saline spray bottle for graft hydration during the first week, our clinic-formulated sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner with panthenol, sterile non-woven scalp pads, a silicone-based scar gel for the donor area, a loose breathable post-op cap, oral pain relief tablets, a 14-day supply of anti-inflammatory medication, printed day-by-day washing instructions, and a QR code linking to video tutorials. International patients also receive a travel-sized version approved for carry-on luggage.
How does microneedle implantation minimise pressure on my existing native hairs?
Three design factors work together. First, the 0.6-0.8mm needle diameter is narrower than standard punches, so less tissue is displaced around each entry point. Second, the implant pen slides grafts into the incision rather than forcing them, eliminating the blunt trauma that can crush adjacent follicles. Third, our surgeons follow a staggered zigzag insertion pattern that avoids compressing the same row twice. When combined, these measures keep shock loss of native hair consistently below 4% based on our internal audit data.
On which day after surgery can I safely wear a fitted hat or baseball cap?
For the first 48 hours we recommend no headwear at all so that the grafts can stabilise undisturbed. From day 3 through day 7 you can wear a very loose, clean bucket hat or surgical cap that sits gently without rubbing your recipient area. From day 8 onward a normal baseball cap is fine, but please wash it first and avoid wearing it for more than 4 consecutive hours at a time. Helmets, beanies, and tight headbands should wait until the 2-week mark to prevent any risk of graft dislodgement or pressure-induced redness.
Is there any size or grade of baldness where Barley's microneedle option is not recommended?
Microneedle FUE works excellently for Norwood grades 2 through 6, which covers the vast majority of patients we see. For very advanced Norwood 7 cases with extremely limited donor supply, our surgeons may recommend combining microneedle FUE with a small FUT strip harvested first, or using beard/body hair supplementation. During consultation we map your permanent donor density using digital microscopy and will tell you honestly whether microneedle FUE alone, a combined approach, or even conservative medical management makes the most sense for your realistic goals.
