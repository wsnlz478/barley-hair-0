Frequently Asked Questions

Organized by phase: booking through to revision, with honest answers you won't find in marketing copy

How soon should I reserve my surgery calendar slot, and what deposit amount holds my date

Peak-season surgeon blocks (Mon-Wed international patient days) are typically fully booked 7-10 weeks ahead, while Thu-Fri and weekend supplementary slots stay open 2-5 weeks out. A 72-hour temporary hold is available on any available slot with a refundable $300 USD security deposit; cancellations posted earlier than 30 days pre-surgery receive the deposit back on the original card, and cancellations inside 30 days convert it to surgical credit. The remaining balance is only collected on surgery morning, after you meet the surgeon in-clinic and lock in the final graft count together.

Will Barley prepare a stamped medical invitation for my visa application and what turnaround can I expect

Absolutely — once your 15% booking confirmation deposit clears we generate a hospital-stamped, surgeon-signed medical invitation letter on registered clinic letterhead. Electronic PDF delivery is 24-48 working hours, and a physical DHL hard copy (required by a few embassies) ships within 3 business days. The paperwork lists your named operating surgeon, confirmed procedure date, and our clinic's Ministry of Health license number. Most passport holders receive a 90-day single-entry medical visa with this documentation alone; we also attach a paid-deposit invoice if your consulate requests additional proof.

Which supplements and medications require a pause at the 1-week, 2-week, and 4-week marks before my procedure

Four weeks out: hold oral finasteride or dutasteride only if you intend to do same-day PRP with autologous blood draw; otherwise oral DHT blockers can continue unchanged. Two weeks out: discontinue fish oil / omega-3 capsules, high-dose vitamin E, all NSAIDs (aspirin, ibuprofen, naproxen), garlic concentrate pills, and green tea extract caplets. One week out: stop topical minoxidil application on both donor and recipient scalp, and either quit nicotine entirely or drop to fewer than 5 cigarettes per day (nicotine constricts scalp vessels and measurably lowers graft take rate). A printable two-page color-coded checklist is emailed within 24 hours of booking.

Must I complete pre-surgery blood labs in my home country, or does the clinic run the full panel on arrival

All mandatory preoperative blood screening is performed in-house at our partner university-hospital laboratory on arrival day one, roughly 24 hours before your scheduled procedure — there is no requirement to run any tests ahead of time unless you have a pre-existing chronic condition. The panel includes complete blood count with platelet quantification, PT/INR coagulation study, hepatitis B surface antigen, hepatitis C antibody, HIV antigen/antibody combo, and a random capillary glucose check. All-in cost for the full panel is $90 USD, collected at check-in. If you already carry a stamped PDF of results (less than 30 days old) from an accredited lab at home, bring it with you and we will waive the lab fee entirely. If INR results exceed 1.3 on test day we will discuss rescheduling options.

Am I allowed a normal breakfast, morning coffee, and routine prescription pills on surgery day

Definitely — we actively encourage a full, hearty breakfast because local-only anesthesia administered on an empty stomach frequently triggers dizziness or vasovagal episodes. Coffee or tea is perfectly acceptable in reasonable amounts (one or two standard cups); please skip caffeinated energy drinks. Take every one of your long-term maintenance prescriptions exactly as directed — hypertension meds, thyroid hormone, diabetes pills, SSRIs, birth control, asthma inhalers, etc. — with a small sip of water. The only medication to skip that morning is a routine NSAID dose you might normally take for headaches or chronic pain. We will double-verify your medication list with you on arrival anyway.

Is one guest allowed inside the surgical rooms with me, or are they restricted to the patient waiting areas only

One designated support person is warmly welcomed in your private pre-op preparation room and in the dedicated post-op patient recovery lounge for the entirety of your procedure day, but cannot enter the sterile extraction or sterile implantation operating rooms — this is a standard infection-control rule across every accredited surgical facility worldwide. We provide your guest with a padded lounge chair, high-speed Wi-Fi password, USB charger, and complimentary hot tea / coffee / bottled water all day, and they are welcome to chat with you during every scheduled break. We build 15-minute rest breaks into the schedule every 90 minutes so you can walk, stretch, use the washroom, and catch up with your guest at any point. Children under 14 are not permitted in the surgical wing at any time for safety reasons.

On which exact post-op days can I normally shampoo, return to exercise, wear a fitted hat, and trim transplanted hair

Post-op day 3: only the clinic-supplied medicated shampoo poured gently from a cup onto your scalp — zero rubbing, scratching, or fingernail contact. Post-op day 10: you may switch to your own regular shampoo and use very light fingertip pressure while showering. Post-op day 14: a loose-fitting woven cotton baseball cap is permitted for outdoor sun protection only; tight compression caps, beanies, and snapbacks stay off until day 30. Light gym cardio returns day 21, heavy resistance training and CrossFit-style lifts day 30. A barber can cut and style the transplanted zone normally starting at month 4, once new hair shafts have hardened sufficiently. We send this timeline as a printable refrigerator-sticker PDF.

What exactly is post-op shock loss, what timeline does it follow, and how can I distinguish it from real graft failure

Shock loss describes the temporary, stress-induced shedding of the transplanted hair fibers (the visible keratin shafts only, NOT the living dermal papilla follicle root anchored beneath the scalp). It affects approximately 60-70% of our patients, beginning anywhere between post-op day 14 and day 30, and resolves completely by week 8-10 in 95% of cases. Normal shock loss vs real graft failure: shed shock-loss hairs show a tiny white keratin bulb at the root end exactly like naturally shed hair, you will never see empty punched pits or red craters on your scalp, and any density reduction appears evenly distributed rather than in sharply defined patches. Regrowth from the unaffected follicle roots restarts reliably at months 3-4.

What services and products are covered in my written quote, what is billed separately, and will my insurer accept the invoice

All written quotes always include: attending surgeon professional fee, full 4-person surgical team compensation, local tumescent anesthesia, individually wrapped single-use sterile instruments, all suture and closure materials, 7 days of oral antibiotics + anti-inflammatories + pain medication, the complete aftercare product kit (medicated shampoo, healing lotion, saline spray, stretch headband), 4 trichoscope scan follow-ups at months 1, 3, 6, and 12, plus the signed written graft-survival guarantee. Charged separately: international flights, hotel stays, meals, on-arrival blood work panel ($90), optional PRP add-on sessions ($220 each), and any future revision surgery. We issue a stamped, CPT-coded line-item invoice in English that FSA/HSA accounts and most private international insurers accept for reimbursement; US HSA plans typically reimburse 40-60%.

If a medical contraindication is found (heart concern / abnormal labs) after deposit payment, is the refund full or partial

If your procedure is medically contraindicated — supported by either a written physician letter from your home doctor, or by abnormal results on our own in-house blood work, or by a formal ruling from our clinic medical director — you receive a complete refund of your booking deposit minus a flat $150 USD non-refundable administration processing fee. If the cancellation is for personal reasons and you provide more than 30 days written notice, you may choose between 100% credit toward a future procedure (valid 24 months) or a 70% cash refund at your election. Personal-reason cancellations inside 14 days convert the deposit to credit only with no cash refund option, because last-minute surgeon slots are extremely difficult to rebook and we likely turned away another confirmed patient. All refund rules are printed in plain language on page 2 of every surgical booking contract; there are no hidden clauses.

Which airport or station does pickup service cover, what is the transfer cost, and will the driver have my name on a sign

All procedure bookings totaling 2000 grafts or more receive complimentary private sedan round-trip ground transport, both pickup and drop-off, to and from Beijing Capital International (PEK), Beijing Daxing International (PKX), or the high-speed Beijing South Railway Station. For smaller procedures under 2000 grafts the round-trip private sedan rate is a flat $70 USD; alternatively you may use the local DiDi ride-hailing app yourself for approximately $25 one-way if you prefer. Your assigned driver will be waiting curbside at the international arrivals exit holding a clearly printed laminated sign with your full legal name exactly as it appears in your passport, plus the Barley Hair clinic logo. Your mobile messaging contact (WhatsApp or WeChat) is saved in the driver's phone so you can message them directly if customs clearance or baggage retrieval takes longer than expected.

If I return home and want month-6 PRP, do you ship clinic-brand aftercare products and can you refer a local PRP provider

Certainly — we ship our full range of in-clinic formulated hair products worldwide via tracked DHL or EMS courier to 95% of countries, including our proprietary minoxidil 5% foam, compounded finasteride 1mg tablets, ketoconazole 2% anti-dandruff shampoo, and the same post-op sterile saline healing spray we send you home with. A standard 3-month supply pack ships for roughly $35 USD and typically delivers in 7-12 business days depending on destination customs. For PRP booster treatments after you return home, we maintain established referral relationships with partner trichologists and hair restoration clinics in 18 major countries (United States, United Kingdom, Germany, United Arab Emirates, Singapore, Australia, and more) who already know our specific graft protocol, centrifuge settings, and PRP concentration targets so your booster treatment is fully consistent. Simply message your patient coordinator around month 4 to request a local referral.

Will the same native-English interpreter stay with me for the complete 8-hour procedure day without shift changes

Yes, contractually guaranteed: one single assigned interpreter stays with you continuously from morning check-in through final evening bandaging, no rotating shifts or handoffs. Every interpreter on our international patient team has a minimum 3-year hair-transplant-specific terminology curriculum — they correctly distinguish follicular unit vs individual follicle, recipient slit angle vs punch diameter, transection rate vs survival rate, etc. — so the surgeon's technical instructions translate without loss of meaning. In the extremely rare event your assigned translator falls ill within 48 hours of surgery (has happened twice in 5 years), we automatically apply a $300 USD credit to your final surgical balance as compensation.

If I request a density touch-up at the 12-month mark, what per-graft discount applies compared to my initial procedure

Any secondary procedure scheduled within 24 calendar months of the original surgery — whether it is a guarantee-covered revision or an elective density touch-up you simply want — automatically receives a fixed 35% discount off the then-current per-graft published price list. When the touch-up is a documented guarantee revision (trichoscope-proven graft survival below 70% in the mapped recipient area), the 35% discount stacks on top of the zero-surgical-fee guarantee, so your out-of-pocket cost is only sterile supplies and medication: typically $450-$750 USD total for a 1000-1500 graft touch-up. This discount schedule is printed verbatim in every patient surgical contract and is non-negotiable.

Can a revision be performed at a different Barley branch than my original clinic, and does the written guarantee transfer

Absolutely yes — the signed written result guarantee document and the 35% revision discount are both valid and fully transferable across any of our 31 Barley clinic locations nationwide. Your complete confidential patient record, including every trichoscope scan, your exact graft count distribution sheet, the operating surgeon's case notes, and all standardized before/after photography, lives in our HIPAA-equivalent encrypted central cloud system linked to your passport number, so any Barley clinic at any city can pull your entire file in under 2 minutes at the front desk during check-in. We only gently request that your month-12 outcome scan be done at the original operating clinic when feasible, because the same attending surgeon who placed the grafts will assess density changes and scar maturation with the greatest possible accuracy; otherwise any location is perfectly acceptable.

What is the policy for a third potential revision — is there a ceiling on how many times the guarantee applies

The zero-surgical-fee written result guarantee covers exactly one revision procedure, scheduled between month 18 and month 24 post-primary, documented by trichoscope measurement as under 70% graft survival within the originally mapped recipient zones. A third procedure is an extremely rare clinical event — fewer than 1.2% of our patients ever reach this conversation — but if you clinically require or cosmetically desire a third session for further density refinement, scar correction, or coverage extension (and it is NOT a guarantee-qualified failure), the same lifetime 35% revision discount that applied to your second procedure still applies to your third. We will not under any circumstances schedule a fourth or fifth procedure on the same patient if repeat trichoscope mapping shows occipital safe-zone donor density has fallen below 2.2 follicular units per square centimeter; this is a hard clinical safety boundary to protect your long-term donor integrity.
