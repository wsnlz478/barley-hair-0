About Barley FAQ

Learn more about our history, team, and commitment to excellence

How long has Barley Hair Transplant Hospital been in operation
Barley was founded in 2006, making us one of China's oldest and most established specialized hair restoration clinics. We opened our first clinic in Beijing with a team of just three surgeons, and pioneered microneedle transplant technology from day one. Over the past 18+ years, we've grown steadily — never franchising, always expanding through directly managed locations — and have performed more than 100,000 successful procedures. That long track record means we have surgical outcomes data spanning nearly two decades, which is rare in this industry.
What qualifications and certifications do Barley surgeons hold
Every surgeon at Barley is a board-certified plastic surgeon or dermatologist with a minimum of 5 years of clinical experience before they even begin our specialized hair transplant training program. That program lasts 12-18 months and includes observing over 300 procedures and assisting on 200+ more before going independent. Many of our lead surgeons are members of the ISHRS (International Society of Hair Restoration Surgery), and several have been invited speakers at international hair restoration conferences.
How many clinic locations does Barley have and where are they
We currently operate 31 directly managed clinics across China's major cities — all company-owned, never franchised, so quality stays consistent. You'll find us in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Chengdu, Chongqing, Hangzhou, Wuhan, Nanjing, Xi'an, Tianjin, Suzhou, Changsha, Shenyang, Qingdao, Dalian, Jinan, Harbin, Zhengzhou, Kunming, Fuzhou, Nanning, Guiyang, Taiyuan, Nanchang, Hefei, Shijiazhuang, Wuxi, Wenzhou, Foshan, Ningbo, and more. We intentionally cover tier-1 and tier-2 cities so domestic patients don't have to travel far.
What international certifications does Barley hold
Barley holds 8 independent Sullivan certifications — the gold standard for medical quality assessment in hair restoration. These cover areas including surgical safety protocols, patient outcomes tracking, microneedle technique excellence, international patient services, and clinic environmental standards. All certifications are audited annually by independent third parties. On top of that, we hold 14 government-issued technology patents for our instruments and methods.
Is there full English-language support for international patients
Yes, completely. Every international patient gets a dedicated English-speaking coordinator assigned to them from the first message — someone who stays with them through consultation, procedure, recovery, and follow-up. All medical paperwork, consent forms, pre-op instructions, and aftercare guides are professionally translated into English. Many of our surgeons speak conversational to fluent English, so consultations often don't even require a translator. We're available on WhatsApp, WeChat, and email 7 days a week.
How does Barley protect patient privacy and confidentiality
Patient privacy is treated with the utmost seriousness. All medical records are stored on encrypted, access-controlled servers with role-based permissions — only your direct care team can view your files. Before-and-after photos are never shared publicly without written, signed consent, and we offer private consultation rooms at every location. For patients who prefer extra discretion, we provide separate entrances, unmarked recovery areas, and no-shave procedures that make post-op detection virtually impossible. We never sell or share any patient data, period.
Are Barley's 14 patents really exclusive, or are they industry-standard tools
They are genuinely exclusive and were developed in-house by our engineering and surgical teams. Our flagship 360° rotating microneedle implant pen, for example, is something we designed, tested, patented, and manufacture ourselves — you won't find it at any other clinic. The patents cover everything from extraction tools to graft storage solutions to angle-specific implantation guides. This isn't marketing fluff; during a consultation you can actually hold the instruments and see the patent documentation if you're curious.
Does Barley do medical research or just perform procedures
Research is actually a huge part of what we do. We run an internal R&D department with 12 biomedical engineers and clinicians who are constantly refining our techniques and equipment. Our teams publish regularly in both Chinese and international medical journals, and we've presented our microneedle survival rate data at three consecutive ISHRS World Congresses. Several of our surgeons also teach training workshops for younger doctors looking to learn our methods. We believe the best clinics aren't just using technology — they're creating it.
