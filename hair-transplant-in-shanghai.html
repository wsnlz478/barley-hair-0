Common Hair Transplant Techniques

Understanding the different methods available for hair restoration in Shanghai

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)

FUE removes individual follicular units from the donor area and implants them into the recipient area.

  • Minimal linear scarring
  • Faster healing time
  • Suitable for short hairstyles
  • Natural-looking results

FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation)

FUT removes a linear strip of scalp from the donor area, from which grafts are dissected.

  • High graft yield in single session
  • Cost-effective for large areas
  • Suitable for advanced baldness
  • Proven long-term results

Microneedle Transplant

Advanced technique using precision microneedle implant pens for superior results.

  • Higher precision placement
  • Better graft survival rate
  • Minimal discomfort
  • Barley's proprietary technology

Choosing the Right Clinic and Surgeon in Shanghai

Key factors to consider when selecting a hair transplant provider in Shanghai

Credentials and Experience

Verify surgeon qualifications, board certifications, and years of focused hair-restoration experience. High-volume experience correlates with predictable graft survival and natural-looking hairlines.

Before-and-After Portfolios

Ask for unedited before-and-after photos of patients with similar hair loss patterns and ethnic hair types. Request contactable patient references when possible.

Techniques and Team

Confirm whether the surgeon personally performs key steps (extraction, recipient site creation, implantation) and whether a trained team supports the operation.

Accreditation and Hygiene

Look for hospital or clinic accreditation, transparent infection-control protocols, and modern operating-room standards.

Transparent Pricing

Ensure itemized estimates that clearly show what's included (consultation, surgery, anesthesia, follow-ups, medications). Avoid hidden fees.

Communication and Support

Choose a clinic with English-speaking staff or professional translation services. Clear communication is essential for your safety and satisfaction.

Recovery Timeline and Aftercare

What to expect during your hair transplant recovery journey in Shanghai

Days 1-3

Immediate Post-Op

Scabbing, mild swelling, and tenderness are common. Follow clinic guidance on cleaning, sleeping position, and activity restrictions to protect grafts.

Weeks 1-2

Early Healing

Scabs fall off; transplanted hairs often shed (shock loss) before a new growth phase begins. Continue prescribed topical treatments or oral medications.

Months 3-6

Hair Growth Phase

New hair appears gradually; substantial cosmetic improvement is typically visible around 6 months and continues to mature up to 12-18 months.

Long-Term

Maintenance

Some patients require additional sessions to increase density. Medical therapies like finasteride and minoxidil are commonly used to preserve native hair.

Shanghai Clinic FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our Shanghai clinic location, international travel, and services

Where is your Shanghai clinic located, and which subway station is closest?

Our Barley Shanghai clinic is located at Plaza 66 Tower II, 1266 Nanjing West Road, Jing'an District. The closest subway station is Nanjing West Road Station on Lines 2, 12, and 13 (Exit 12), approximately a 4-minute walk through the underground mall. The clinic sits directly above the luxury shopping district, with easy access to the Bund via Line 2 (3 stops east to East Nanjing Road). You can also walk 15 minutes to People's Square for additional lines.

What are the opening hours for the Shanghai clinic?

Our Shanghai clinic operates Monday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 8:30 PM, including most national holidays. Surgical procedures are typically scheduled between 8:30 AM and 4:30 PM, with evening consultations and follow-ups available until 8:00 PM for the convenience of working professionals in the Lujiazui and Jing'an business districts. We recommend booking preferred surgery dates at least 5-7 days in advance due to high demand.

How do I travel from Pudong Airport or Hongqiao Airport to the Shanghai clinic?

From Pudong International (PVG): Take the Maglev Train to Longyang Road (8 minutes), then transfer to Metro Line 2 westbound toward East Xujing and ride 9 stops to Nanjing West Road (~45 min total). From Hongqiao International (SHA): Take Metro Line 2 eastbound directly 8 stops to Nanjing West Road Station (~30 min). We offer complimentary private airport transfer for international patients booking 2,000+ graft packages — simply request when scheduling your consultation.

What travel advice do you give patients from Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and other East China provinces?

Patients from Suzhou, Wuxi, Nanjing, Hangzhou, Ningbo, and Hefei typically arrive via Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station on the high-speed rail network (Suzhou is only 25 minutes away!). From Hongqiao Station walk directly to Metro Line 2 and take it 8 stops east to Nanjing West Road. We recommend staying 3 nights for standard procedures. Patients from Shandong, Fujian, and Jiangxi can fly into Hongqiao on domestic discount routes. Remember to bring light, loose-fitting button-up shirts and avoid Shanghai's humid summer midday sun outdoors the first week.

What surgical team qualifications and quality standards does Barley follow for hair restoration procedures?

Barley maintains strict qualification standards across all our clinic locations. Every lead surgeon must hold a valid Chinese medical license with at least 8 years of specialized hair restoration experience, a minimum track record of 3,000 completed transplant procedures, and documented training in our proprietary microneedle implantation system. Each surgical team includes a licensed extraction surgeon, 2-4 trained graft placement technicians, and a dedicated circulating sterile assistant. Our internal audit committee reviews every surgeon's survival rate data, patient satisfaction scores, and complication reports quarterly. Before your procedure, we are happy to share the surgical team's collective experience profile, average graft survival metrics, and anonymized before/after portfolio samples from cases similar to your planned session.

Which hotels do you recommend near the Shanghai clinic?

Three tiers near Nanjing West Road and the Bund: Luxury option — The Portman Ritz-Carlton Shanghai shares the same Plaza 66 complex as our clinic, literally an elevator ride away (~$250/night, concierge direct clinic access). Mid-range — The Middle House on Shaanxi South Road (6-minute walk, ~$136/night, boutique design hotel). Budget-friendly — Hanting Premium Nanjing West Road (3-minute subway exit, ~$58/night). Patients booking through our concierge receive negotiated 18-25% discount rates, plus guaranteed late checkout the day after surgery.

Is there a visa-free transit policy for foreigners visiting Shanghai?

Absolutely — both Pudong and Hongqiao Airports, along with Shanghai Port and Shanghai Railway Station, participate in China's 144-hour visa-free transit policy for 53 eligible countries including all EU nations, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, and New Zealand. You may spend up to 6 full days within the Shanghai-Jiangsu-Zhejiang region without a traditional visa as long as you hold confirmed onward tickets to a third country. For longer stays or business visas, the clinic can issue a formal invitation letter with Jiao Tong University medical affiliation to support L-visa or M-visa applications at any Chinese consulate worldwide.
