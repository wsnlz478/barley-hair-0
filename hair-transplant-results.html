Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers about real-world results, density, worst-case scenarios, and how to verify outcomes

What's the worst-case result I can realistically expect if everything goes only averagely
With a healthy donor and a stable NW3-NW5 pattern, the lower bound is typically 65-75% coverage at 12 months, meaning your bald spot is visually reduced but not fully gone, and you still style with some product to cover. For patients with thinner donor density or NW6-NW7 pattern, worst case is usually 50-60% of your goal density - still a cosmetic improvement but not a full restoration. We never promise "90% to every patient; the written consultation note lists your personal best and worst range based on your scalp analysis.
If I'm genuinely unhappy with my final density, can I get a free touch-up session
Yes, within the Result Guarantee terms: if at 12 months the independent trichoscope scan shows under 70% graft survival in the recipient area AND you followed all post-op care rules documented in the app and kept all follow-up appointments, you qualify for a complimentary revision up to 1500 grafts, free of surgical fee - you only cover consumables and meds. You must bring your signed post-op photos every 3 months for the guarantee to apply.
What kind of patients typically need a second transplant, and how often does that happen at Barley
Second sessions happen in about 12-15% of Barley patients, usually one of four groups: NW5-NW7 advanced baldness with limited donor supply who wants higher density; patients with coarse curly hair where initial 1st pass camouflage needs a 2nd pass; vertex crowns because the swirl zone is harder to thicken in one go; and patients who keep losing native hair the first 3 years after surgery without finasteride/minoxidil. The 2nd session is usually spaced 12-18 months after the first, never earlier, priced at a 25-35% discount vs the first.
Are the before and after photos on the results page shot with the same lighting, camera and angle
Yes - every paired case photo follows the exact same protocol: Canon 6D Mark II, 50mm f/8, color temperature 5500K studio lights from the same two softbox positions, same distance 1.4m, same four fixed angles front / 45 degrees side / top-down, same wet-hair protocol before blow-dry to the same length with the same comb, 10-day minimum hair length. We do not use barbershop lights, bathroom mirrors, or outdoor natural light on result photos. A timestamp and patient ID is embedded in every EXIF.
If some of my non-transplanted native hairs shed 2-4 years after surgery, who covers fixing that zone
Native hair miniaturization and shedding post-surgery happens to about 30% of men who don't use DHT-blocking meds long-term. If the shed hairs are outside your original graft recipient border zone, that's a separate progression, not a graft failure, and not covered by the free touch-up. We recommend all male patients use 1mg finasteride daily + 5% minoxidil 2x/day indefinitely to lock in the native hairs around the transplant border, or 2x/year PRP maintenance as an alternative. Add-on sessions for native loss are discounted.
How do you prove the "90%+ survival rate - can I verify my own graft counts and survival at 12 months
We use the HairMetrix trichoscope pre-op and at 3 / 6 / 12 months - it counts follicular unit density per cm2 non-invasively down to 0.1mm depth. On surgery day you receive a signed graft tally sheet from the graft counter technician, real grafts extracted, real grafts implanted by zone. At 12 months we re-scan the identical cm2 grid and compare. If you don't trust in-house numbers you can pay a local independent trichologist for a second scan at month 12 for about $120-200 and we will reimburse if our internal vs external numbers differ by more than 8%.
Can I get an independent third-party result validation before I pay the final surgery balance
For international patients, you pay only 15% deposit to book, the rest is day of surgery after you meet the surgeon and confirm graft count sheet. Post-surgery there's no independent auditor in the room during the procedure itself, but at 12 months we accept reports from any board-certified dermatologist / trichologist not affiliated with Barley anywhere in the world for our Result Guarantee. No third-party signed document is required to claim the touch-up; only the trichoscope scans and documented compliance to your own 3, 6, 9, 12 month selfies in the patient app are required.
