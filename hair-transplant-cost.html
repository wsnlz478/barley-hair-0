Why China Offers Better Value

The price difference is not about quality —it is about economics. China's lower operating costs, favourable exchange rates, and government support for medical tourism mean that clinics can offer premium procedures at accessible prices. Barley Hospital invests heavily in the latest technology, continuous surgeon training, and state-of-the-art facilities —the same investments that drive up prices in Western clinics.

Additionally, our surgeons perform hundreds of procedures per year, achieving exceptional skill levels that rival the best practitioners worldwide. Higher surgical volume translates to better outcomes at lower costs —a benefit that is passed directly to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Exact answers about what is included, deposits, refunds, instalments, and invoicing

Does the quoted price include medications, disposables, and the post-op care kit
Yes, 100%. The written quote includes the surgeon fee, the surgical team, local anaesthesia, extraction and implantation instruments, sterile drapes and gloves, the 5-7 day course of antibiotics and anti-inflammatories, painkillers if needed, and the full aftercare kit (saline spray, specialised shampoo, scalp lotion, and the surgical cap). Blood tests required before surgery are also covered. The only things not included are flights, hotel, and your personal spending - and we list those clearly in the pre-trip summary so nothing is ambiguous.
How much is the booking deposit, and in what scenarios is it refundable
The standard booking deposit is 20% of the total procedure quote, which locks your surgeon, translator, and surgery date. It is fully refundable up to 21 days before your scheduled surgery if you need to cancel for any reason - no questions asked, and the money returns to the same card or account you paid from within 5 business days. If you need to reschedule instead of cancelling, the deposit transfers directly to your new date with zero penalty or fee.
What are the exact terms of the instalment or payment plan options
We offer two standard plans with zero interest and no admin fee. Plan A is 3 payments: 20% deposit on booking, 40% when you arrive and approve the in-person consultation, and 40% the day before surgery. Plan B, available for packages over $4,000, spreads it across 4 equal payments over 6 weeks from the booking date. There is no credit check and no late fee as long as you notify the team 3 days before a scheduled payment if you need to shift it by a week or less.
Who pays international bank transfer fees, and what is the cheapest way to pay from abroad
You are responsible for the sending-side fee your bank or Wise/Revolut charges, but we cover all receiving-side intermediary and conversion fees on our end - so the exact amount you send is the exact amount that lands, no shortfall surprises. For most patients, Wise or a SEPA transfer in EUR gives the lowest cost (typically $5-$15 total). For credit card payments, Visa and Mastercard are accepted with a standard 2.5% international card processing fee, which the coordinator will show you explicitly before you confirm.
Are there discounts for a spouse, sibling, or friend booking the same trip
Yes. A spouse, partner, sibling, or friend who travels on the same dates and books a procedure of 1,500 grafts or more gets 8% off their own quote automatically, and you both receive a complimentary PRP session after surgery. If they are just accompanying you without a procedure, there is no discount, but we comp their airport transfer and secure the same or adjacent hotel room at the partner rate - usually 20-30% below the public price the hotel charges directly.
How much does a revision or touch-up cost if I had a botched transplant elsewhere
Failed-repair cases are priced individually because they depend on the scar tissue condition, how many usable grafts your donor area still has, and whether the previous clinic used FUT (strip) or FUE. As a guide, small corrective sessions of 800-1,200 grafts usually range from $840 to $3,900, while full coverage repairs of 2,000+ grafts are typically $1,960 to $7,700. The first repair consultation is always free, including a photo-based estimate you can compare against before you travel.
Can I get a proper tax invoice or receipt, in English, for insurance or reimbursement claims
Absolutely. Within 3 business days after you make your final payment, the accounts team emails you a formal English-language invoice showing the clinic tax ID, the procedure codes, itemised breakdown, date, surgeon name, and clinic seal in PDF. If your insurer or health fund needs a specific format - for example, a CMS 1500-style form or a letter on clinic letterhead - just forward the template to your coordinator and they will prepare it at no extra charge. Many patients use these to claim back a portion from their private extras cover.
