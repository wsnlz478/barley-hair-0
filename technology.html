FUE Technique

FUE Hair Transplant

Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) is the gold standard in modern hair restoration. Individual follicular units are harvested from the donor area using precision micro-punches, then implanted with our microneedle pen for natural-looking results with virtually no visible scarring.

Minimally invasive procedure
No linear scarring
Quick recovery time
Natural hair growth

FUT Technique

FUT Hair Transplant

Follicular Unit Transplantation (FUT) involves harvesting a strip of donor tissue, which is then meticulously dissected into individual follicular units. While FUE is our primary recommendation, FUT remains a viable option for patients requiring maximum graft counts in a single session.

Higher graft yield
Cost-effective for large areas
Proven effectiveness
Expert surgical technique

Our Hair Transplant Process

Your complete 9-step journey to natural hair restoration

01

Consultation & Scalp Assessment

Your surgeon evaluates your scalp, grades your hair loss, and assesses donor area follicle density to confirm you're a good candidate for transplantation.

02

Personalized Hairline Design

Your surgeon hand-draws a customized hairline that complements your facial proportions, age, and personal preferences. We plan every implantation zone and calculate the exact graft count needed.

03

Pre-Procedure Health Screening

Standard blood work and coagulation tests to ensure there are no medical concerns. Your safety is our first priority.

04

Local Anesthesia

Targeted numbing of the donor and recipient areas. You'll stay awake and comfortable throughout, with no pain during the procedure.

05

Donor Follicle Harvesting

Individual follicular units are carefully extracted from the donor area at the back of your head using precision micro-punches that leave only tiny, virtually invisible marks.

06

Graft Preparation & Sorting

Our technicians carefully clean, sort, and store harvested follicles in a temperature-controlled nutrient solution to keep them at peak viability until implantation.

07

Precision Graft Implantation

Healthy follicles are meticulously placed one by one using our patented microneedle pen. The 360 degree rotation ensures each graft matches the natural growth direction of surrounding hair.

08

Immediate Post-Procedure Care

The scalp is gently cleaned and fitted with a light protective dressing. We make sure you're comfortable and provide detailed washing instructions before you leave.

09

Aftercare & Follow-Up

You'll receive comprehensive written instructions on washing, daily activities, and what to expect during recovery. We schedule follow-up check-ins to monitor your progress every step of the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our microneedle hair transplant technology

What is the core difference between microneedle implantation and traditional FUE forceps placement
Traditional FUE creates a recipient slit with a blade or needle first, then a second technician manipulates the graft into that slit using jeweler's forceps — a two-step process where the graft can get crushed, folded, or seated too shallow or too deep. With the microneedle implant pen, incision and placement happen in a single coordinated motion. The graft is loaded inside the hollow needle tip, and as the surgeon pierces the scalp, a plunger deposits the follicle at the exact calibrated depth. This cuts handling time per graft from roughly eight seconds to under three seconds, drastically improving survival.
How many proprietary patents does Barley hold, and what do they cover
We hold 14 registered patents spanning the entire surgical workflow. Key patents include the 360-degree rotating implant pen mechanism, the adjustable depth-stop collar for consistent implantation depth, the 4-degree Celsius cold-chain storage cassettes that maintain graft viability outside the body, the scalp stabilization ring for atraumatic donor extraction, the intelligent density-spacing algorithm, and the accelerated recovery aftercare protocol. Each patent represents a deliberate engineering decision aimed at improving survival rates and aesthetic outcomes.
What punch sizes are used during extraction and implantation, and why does it matter
Our standard extraction punches range from 0.8mm for finer-textured hair up to 1.0mm for coarse curly hair. On the implantation side, the microneedle pen tips span 0.6mm to 1.0mm depending on body region and hair caliber — eyebrows and the frontal hairline typically use 0.6mm tips, while mid-scalp and coarser donor grafts use 0.8mm or 1.0mm. The punch size directly impacts scarring: smaller diameters create dot scars that become invisible once healed, whereas larger 1.2mm+ punches can leave permanent cobblestone texture.
Can the microneedle system successfully grow hair inside scar tissue from burns or old injuries
Yes, in most cases. Scar tissue has reduced vascularity, so we adjust the protocol slightly: we use 0.8mm punches instead of 0.6mm to create slightly wider recipient channels that encourage blood-vessel in-growth, and we combine the transplant with our FGF dual-factor growth-factor treatment during implantation. Pre-operative scar assessment is critical — we test the scar bed with a small trial graft zone during the initial consultation whenever possible to confirm viability before committing to a full procedure.
What extraction tools and donor harvesting methods does Barley use
Donor follicles are harvested using computer-calibrated motorized FUE punches that oscillate at a controlled RPM rather than manual punches. This oscillating motion scores the dermis cleanly without twisting or stretching the follicle, reducing transection rates. Extraction is always performed by trained surgeons rather than unlicensed technicians, and we spread extraction across the entire safe donor zone to preserve overall donor density rather than strip-mining a single band at the nape.
Is the implant pen genuinely better than the Choi implanter pen or forceps-based DHI
We believe so, and here's why. Standard implanter pens like the Choi tool have a fixed barrel that cannot rotate during placement, which means the graft naturally aligns to whatever angle the pen entered at rather than your actual hair-growth angle. The Choi needle also starts at approximately 0.7mm minimum, while our smallest tip is 0.6mm. Our pen's patented rotating barrel lets the surgeon physically twist each graft to match the native angle as it's deposited, and the depth collar locks in a consistent 1.0mm seating depth for every single follicle.
How does the cold-chain graft preservation system actually improve survival rates
Once a follicle is extracted from the scalp, its metabolic clock is ticking — every minute spent outside the body raises the risk of graft necrosis. Our system stores each tray of grafts in a refrigerated nutrient bath held between 4 and 6 degrees Celsius, which slows cellular metabolism to a near-dormant state. The cassettes are also sealed with a humidity barrier to prevent drying. In practice, this extends the safe ex-vivo window from approximately two hours to well over six hours, which is especially critical for large sessions of 3,500 grafts and up.
How does the AI-assisted design tool work, and what does the surgeon still control manually
The AI system takes calibrated facial photographs and maps key landmarks — brow position, glabella, nose tip, lip line, chin — to recommend a mathematically ideal hairline height and temple recession based on your age, ethnicity, and facial proportions. However, the final design is always drawn on your scalp in ink by your operating surgeon, who overlays the AI suggestion with artistic adjustments for softness, asymmetry, and natural hair swirl. We've never run a case where the surgeon used the AI output unchanged.
