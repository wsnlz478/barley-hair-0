Common Hair Transplant Techniques

Advanced options designed for Nanshan tech professionals seeking fast recovery

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)

FUE removes individual follicular units from the donor area and implants them into the recipient area.

  • Minimal linear scarring
  • Back to office in 3-4 days
  • Suitable for short hairstyles
  • Natural-looking hairlines

FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation)

FUT removes a linear strip of scalp from the donor area, from which grafts are dissected.

  • High graft yield single session
  • Cost-effective for large areas
  • Suitable for Norwood 5-7
  • Proven long-term results

Microneedle Transplant

Advanced precision implant pens — our most popular choice for HK and tech professionals.

  • Higher precision placement
  • Better graft survival rate
  • Minimal downtime for 996 workers
  • Barley proprietary technology

Choosing the Right Clinic and Surgeon in Shenzhen

Key factors for Hong Kong commuters and Nanshan Hi-Tech Park professionals

Surgeon Credentials & Volume

Verify surgeon qualifications, board certifications, and years of focused hair-restoration experience. High-volume experience is critical for predictable graft survival in single-day procedures designed for commuters.

Before/After Portfolios

Ask for unedited before-and-after photos of patients with similar hair loss patterns and ethnic hair types — especially Hong Kong Chinese patients if you're coming across the border. Request reference contacts when possible.

Fast-Track Process

Confirm whether the surgeon personally performs extraction, site creation, and implantation. Ensure the team can complete your procedure in a single day so you can return to Hong Kong or work on Monday.

Accreditation & Hygiene

Look for hospital or clinic accreditation, transparent infection-control protocols, and modern OR standards. Our clinic maintains full operating licenses inspected annually by the Shenzhen Health Commission.

Transparent All-In Pricing

Ensure itemized estimates that clearly show what's included — consultation, surgery, anesthesia, HK follow-up protocol, medications. Beware clinics that quote per graft only then add mandatory "service fees."

Border-Friendly Logistics

Choose a clinic within 30 minutes of Futian or Luohu border crossings with metro access. Confirm availability of Cantonese-speaking staff, Hong Kong payment methods (FPS/AlipayHK), and post-op remote follow-up by WhatsApp.

144-Hour Visa-Free Transit: Citizens of 53 countries (EU, UK, US, Canada, Australia, NZ, Japan, Korea and more) qualify for 6-day visa-free entry when arriving through Shenzhen Bao'an Airport or any Guangdong port with confirmed onward international ticket within 144 hours. Hong Kong & Macau Tour Group (144h Visa-Free): HK/Macau permanent residents traveling with a registered tour group (even just 2 people) also qualify for 144-hour visa-free stays in Guangdong. Our partner visa agency can handle documentation within 48 hours of booking.
