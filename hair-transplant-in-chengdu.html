Common Hair Transplant Techniques

Understanding the different methods available for hair restoration in Chengdu

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)

FUE removes individual follicular units from the donor area and implants them into the recipient area.

  • Minimal linear scarring
  • Faster healing time
  • Suitable for short hairstyles
  • Natural-looking results

FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation)

FUT removes a linear strip of scalp from the donor area, from which grafts are dissected.

  • High graft yield in single session
  • Cost-effective for large areas
  • Suitable for advanced baldness
  • Proven long-term results

Microneedle Transplant

Advanced technique using precision microneedle implant pens for superior results.

  • Higher precision placement
  • Better graft survival rate
  • Minimal discomfort
  • Barley's proprietary technology

Choosing the Right Clinic and Surgeon in Chengdu

Key factors to consider when selecting a hair transplant provider in Chengdu

Credentials and Experience

Verify surgeon qualifications, board certifications, and years of focused hair-restoration experience. High-volume experience correlates with predictable graft survival and natural-looking hairlines.

Before-and-After Portfolios

Ask for unedited before-and-after photos of patients with similar hair loss patterns and ethnic hair types. Request contactable patient references when possible.

Techniques and Team

Confirm whether the surgeon personally performs key steps (extraction, recipient site creation, implantation) and whether a trained team supports the operation.

Accreditation and Hygiene

Look for hospital or clinic accreditation, transparent infection-control protocols, and modern operating-room standards.

Transparent Pricing

Ensure itemized estimates that clearly show what's included (consultation, surgery, anesthesia, follow-ups, medications). Avoid hidden fees.

Communication and Support

Choose a clinic with English-speaking staff or professional translation services. Clear communication is essential for your safety and satisfaction.

Recovery Timeline and Aftercare

What to expect during your hair transplant recovery journey in Chengdu

Days 1-3

Immediate Post-Op

Scabbing, mild swelling, and tenderness are common. Follow clinic guidance on cleaning, sleeping position, and activity restrictions to protect grafts.

Weeks 1-2

Early Healing

Scabs fall off; transplanted hairs often shed (shock loss) before a new growth phase begins. Continue prescribed topical treatments or oral medications.

Months 3-6

Hair Growth Phase

New hair appears gradually; substantial cosmetic improvement is typically visible around 6 months and continues to mature up to 12-18 months.

Long-Term

Maintenance

Some patients require additional sessions to increase density. Medical therapies like finasteride and minoxidil are commonly used to preserve native hair.

Chengdu Clinic FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our Chengdu Jinjiang clinic near Chunxi Road, travel logistics for Tibet/Yunnan/Guizhou patients, and consular visa services

Where is your Chengdu clinic located, and which subway station is closest?

Our Barley Chengdu clinic is located at Level 18, Tower 3, IFS International Finance Center, 1 Zheng Kejia Alley, Chunxi Road, Jinjiang District. The closest subway stations are Chunxi Road Station on Lines 2 and 3 (Exit C or D), approximately a 5-minute walk through the IFS mall. You can also reach us from Tianfu Square Station via Line 1 — about a 12-minute walk past the iconic panda climbing sculpture. The clinic is surrounded by Sichuan hotpot restaurants, tea houses, and boutique hotels in the heart of the city.

What are the opening hours for the Chengdu clinic?

Our Chengdu Jinjiang clinic operates Monday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, including most national holidays. Surgical procedures are typically scheduled between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM, while consultations and follow-ups can be arranged through 7:00 PM. We offer extended evening hours until 7:30 PM on Tuesdays and Thursdays for Tianfu New Area professionals commuting from the south. Weekend bookings fill up quickly, especially during hotpot festival season — we recommend reserving at least 4 days in advance.

How do I travel from Shuangliu Airport, Tianfu International Airport, or Chengdu East Station to the clinic?

From Shuangliu International Airport: Take Metro Line 10 to Taipingyuan, transfer to Line 3 heading to Chengdu Medical College, and ride 8 stops to Chunxi Road (total ~55 minutes). From Tianfu International Airport: Take the Tianfu Airport Express train to South Railway Station, transfer to Line 1 towards Weijianian, and ride 5 stops to Tianfu Square (total ~70 minutes). From Chengdu East Railway Station (where high-speed trains from Guiyang, Kunming, and Chongqing arrive): Take Metro Line 2 towards Xipu and ride 6 stops directly to Chunxi Road (total ~25 minutes). We offer complimentary private pickup for international patients and anyone booking 2,500+ graft packages — just request it when scheduling.

What travel advice do you give patients from Tibet, Yunnan, Guizhou, or southern Sichuan?

Patients from Lhasa, Xigaze, and other Tibetan areas typically fly into Shuangliu Airport; we recommend arriving 1 full day before surgery to acclimatize to Chengdu's 500-meter altitude after being at 3,000+ meters. Patients from Kunming, Dali, Lijiang, Guiyang, Zunyi, and Panzhihua arrive via Chengdu East Station on the expanded high-speed rail network. We recommend staying at least 4 nights for a standard procedure: acclimatization/consultation day, surgery day, observation day, and a follow-up wash the day after. Winter patients from Tibet and northern Yunnan should bring a light silk scarf for wind protection outdoors during the first week.

What surgical team qualifications and quality standards does Barley follow for hair restoration procedures?

Barley maintains strict qualification standards across all our clinic locations. Every lead surgeon must hold a valid Chinese medical license with at least 8 years of specialized hair restoration experience, a minimum track record of 3,000 completed transplant procedures, and documented training in our proprietary microneedle implantation system. Each surgical team includes a licensed extraction surgeon, 2-4 trained graft placement technicians, and a dedicated circulating sterile assistant. Our internal audit committee reviews every surgeon's survival rate data, patient satisfaction scores, and complication reports quarterly. Before your procedure, we are happy to share the surgical team's collective experience profile, average graft survival metrics, and anonymized before/after portfolio samples from cases similar to your planned session.

Which hotels do you recommend near the Chengdu clinic?

We recommend three tiers near Chunxi Road and Taikoo Li: Luxury option — The Temple House Chengdu by Banyan Tree (literally adjacent to the clinic, ~$315/night, incredible tea-house courtyard). Mid-range — JI Hotel Chunxi Road Branch (6-minute walk through IFS, ~$80/night, clean modern design). Budget-friendly — Home Inn Selected Chunxi Road Taikoo Li Branch (9-minute walk, ~$44/night, right above a famous Sichuan noodle shop). All are within walking distance of the Jinjiang District visa offices, major pharmacies, and endless hotpot options. Patients who book through our concierge receive a negotiated 17-23% discount at partner hotels, plus complimentary late checkout on surgery day.

Is there a visa-free transit policy for foreigners visiting Chengdu, and how convenient are the local consulates?

Yes — Chengdu Shuangliu and Tianfu International Airports both participate in China's 144-hour visa-free transit policy for passport holders from 53 eligible countries including the USA, UK, EU nations, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, and South Korea. You can stay up to 6 full days in the Chengdu-Chongqing-Guiyang cluster without a traditional visa as long as you hold confirmed onward tickets to a third country. Chengdu also hosts one of China's largest consular districts with US, German, French, Australian, Italian, Swiss, and many other consulates located mainly around Tongzilin, Tianfu 2nd Street, and the consular office cluster near Tianfu Square. We can provide an official invitation letter to support your business M-visa or tourist L-visa application, and we work closely with the visa service centers on Tianfu Avenue for expedited processing.
