Common Hair Transplant Techniques

Advanced methods available at our Tianjin Wudadao Heping District clinic — popular for Beijing 1-day return commuters

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)

FUE removes individual follicular units from the donor area and implants them into the recipient area.

  • Minimal linear scarring
  • Faster healing time
  • Suitable for short hairstyles
  • Natural-looking results

FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation)

FUT removes a linear strip of scalp from the donor area, from which grafts are dissected.

  • High graft yield in single session
  • Cost-effective for large areas
  • Suitable for advanced baldness
  • Proven long-term results

Microneedle Transplant

Advanced technique using precision microneedle implant pens for superior results.

  • Higher precision placement
  • Better graft survival rate
  • Minimal discomfort
  • Barley's proprietary technology

Choosing the Right Clinic and Surgeon

Essential factors for Tianjin Beijing commuters, eastern Hebei patients, and Binhai port business travelers

Credentials and Experience

Verify surgeon qualifications, board certifications, and years of focused hair-restoration experience. High-volume experience correlates with predictable graft survival and natural-looking hairlines.

Before-and-After Portfolios

Ask for unedited before-and-after photos of patients with similar hair loss patterns and ethnic hair types. Request contactable patient references when possible.

Techniques and Team

Confirm whether the surgeon personally performs key steps (extraction, recipient site creation, implantation) and whether a trained team supports the operation.

Accreditation and Hygiene

Look for hospital or clinic accreditation, transparent infection-control protocols, and modern operating-room standards.

Transparent Pricing

Ensure itemized estimates that clearly show what's included (consultation, surgery, anesthesia, follow-ups, medications). Avoid hidden fees.

Communication and Support

Choose a clinic with English-speaking staff or professional translation services. Clear communication is essential for your safety and satisfaction.
