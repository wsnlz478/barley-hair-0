A hairline transplant surgically restores your frontal hairline by relocating healthy follicles from the permanent donor zone to the front of your scalp. At Barley, our surgeons use proprietary microneedle FUE technology with 0.6mm micro-punches to extract and implant each follicle at precise 10–15° angles—replicating your natural hair growth pattern. The result is a soft, irregular front edge that is virtually indistinguishable from a natural hairline.

Step by Step: Your Hairline Transplant Journey

1

Design & Planning

Your journey begins with a thorough consultation. Our surgeon examines your scalp, discusses your goals, and draws a preliminary hairline directly on your forehead. We refine the design together until you are completely happy with it. Your forehead height, eyebrow position, and facial symmetry all play a role in shaping the final result.

2

Follicle Extraction

Using our patented microneedle FUE system, individual grafts are carefully harvested from the permanent donor zone at the back of your head. The 0.6mm micro-punch leaves only tiny dots that heal invisibly within days — so you can keep your hair short without anyone noticing.

3

Graft Preparation

The extracted follicles are immediately placed in a specialized preservation solution and sorted under a microscope. Single-hair grafts are set aside for the front hairline, while multi-hair grafts are reserved for the area behind it where more density is needed.

4

Recipient Site Creation

Our surgeon makes micro-incisions along the planned hairline using ultra-fine needles. Each incision is angled at 10–15 degrees to match the natural, flat-lying growth pattern of frontal hair. This step is critical — it is what makes the final result look natural rather than "pluggy."

5

Follicle Placement

Each prepared graft is gently inserted into its designated site. The very front row receives single-hair grafts for a soft, irregular edge, while density builds gradually behind it. Every hair is placed to grow in the correct direction for a seamless, natural look.

6

Recovery & Growth

After the procedure, you receive detailed aftercare instructions. New hair starts growing around months 3–4, with noticeable density by month 6. Your full, mature result appears between 12–18 months — a permanent, natural-looking hairline that lasts a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your procedure

How many hairline design options will the surgeon present during my consultation
During your design consultation, our surgeon typically presents 2 to 4 personalized hairline options based on your facial structure, age, and goals. These usually include a conservative mature hairline, a balanced youthful design, and one or two custom variations (such as a widow's peak or rounded shape). You will see each option drawn directly on your scalp or simulated on photos, and together we refine the final design until you are completely satisfied with the shape, height, and temple details.
What is the typical graft density used at the frontal hairline and temple corners
For natural-looking results, we use a gradient density approach. At the very front hairline edge, density is lighter at approximately 25–35 FU/cm² using only single-hair grafts to create the soft, feathered appearance of a natural hairline. Moving 1 to 2 centimeters back into the frontal zone, density increases to 45–60 FU/cm² with a mix of single and multi-hair grafts. Temple corners follow the same principle but are often slightly lighter, around 20–30 FU/cm² at the edges, to preserve that natural temple recession taper.
Will the designed hairline end up too high and make my face look longer
This is one of our top priorities during the design phase. We measure your forehead from brow to current hairline and use facial thirds analysis — a balanced face has roughly equal distance from hairline to brow, brow to nose tip, and nose tip to chin. Your new hairline is positioned based on these proportions, not an arbitrary height. We also account for future hair loss progression by placing it at a conservative, age-appropriate level rather than aggressively low. The result is a hairline that improves your balance rather than exaggerating a long face.
How much more expensive is a no-shave hairline transplant compared to the shaved method
No-shave (also called unshaven or long-hair) hairline transplants are typically 30% to 50% more expensive than shaved FUE. The price difference reflects the significantly longer time required for both extraction (harvesting individual long hairs without trimming) and placement (inserting grafts through existing hair without damaging nearby follicles). For many patients, the premium is worth it because they can return to work and social settings within days with virtually no visible signs of surgery, especially for hairline-only cases where the donor area can be covered.
What are the technical differences between a female high-forehead hairline and male M-pattern restoration
The two procedures use the same FUE technology but differ substantially in design and placement strategy. Male M-pattern restoration typically rebuilds temple peaks that have receded while maintaining a slightly mature, masculine hairline shape, with focus on the corners. Female high-forehead lowering is usually a flat or gently curved horizontal advance across the entire frontal width with softer, more rounded temples. Female hairlines also require finer single-hair grafts for a less dense but more natural feminine front edge, and the design often preserves or creates a subtle widow's peak for facial contouring.
What face shapes work best with a widow's peak hairline design
A widow's peak (a small V-shaped point at the center of the hairline) complements several face shapes beautifully. It works especially well on round faces, where the V-point adds length and structure to balance circular proportions. Heart-shaped faces typically pair naturally with a widow's peak as it echoes the forehead's natural taper. Oval faces are versatile and can carry a subtle or pronounced peak elegantly. Square or rectangular jaws look best with a softer, less dramatic widow's peak to avoid adding too many sharp angles. We test different peak depths during your consultation to find the most flattering option.
How long after a hairline transplant can I grow bangs or style my hair forward over the forehead
You can begin gently styling the existing (non-transplanted) hair forward as early as two weeks, as long as you avoid putting direct pressure or combing tension on the transplanted graft sites. Most patients can comfortably start wearing bangs or a forward fringe around month 3 to 4, once the transplanted hairs have begun to sprout and anchor more firmly. By month 6, when 50% to 60% of the new hair has emerged, you can style bangs naturally just like before your hair loss. Remember that individual growth rates vary, so we evaluate your progress at each follow-up and advise you on the best timeline for your specific case.
