Common Hair Transplant Techniques

Understanding the different methods available for hair restoration in Nanjing

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)

FUE removes individual follicular units from the donor area and implants them into the recipient area.

  • Minimal linear scarring
  • Faster healing time
  • Suitable for short hairstyles
  • Natural-looking results

FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation)

FUT removes a linear strip of scalp from the donor area, from which grafts are dissected.

  • High graft yield in single session
  • Cost-effective for large areas
  • Suitable for advanced baldness
  • Proven long-term results

Microneedle Transplant

Advanced technique using precision microneedle implant pens for superior results.

  • Higher precision placement
  • Better graft survival rate
  • Minimal discomfort
  • Barley's proprietary technology

Choosing the Right Clinic and Surgeon in Nanjing

Key factors to consider when selecting a hair transplant provider in Nanjing

Credentials and Experience

Verify surgeon qualifications, board certifications, and years of focused hair-restoration experience. High-volume experience correlates with predictable graft survival and natural-looking hairlines.

Before-and-After Portfolios

Ask for unedited before-and-after photos of patients with similar hair loss patterns and ethnic hair types. Request contactable patient references when possible.

Techniques and Team

Confirm whether the surgeon personally performs key steps (extraction, recipient site creation, implantation) and whether a trained team supports the operation.

Accreditation and Hygiene

Look for hospital or clinic accreditation, transparent infection-control protocols, and modern operating-room standards.

Transparent Pricing

Ensure itemized estimates that clearly show what's included (consultation, surgery, anesthesia, follow-ups, medications). Avoid hidden fees.

Communication and Support

Choose a clinic with English-speaking staff or professional translation services. Clear communication is essential for your safety and satisfaction.

Recovery Timeline and Aftercare

What to expect during your hair transplant recovery journey in Nanjing

Days 1-3

Immediate Post-Op

Scabbing, mild swelling, and tenderness are common. Follow clinic guidance on cleaning, sleeping position, and activity restrictions to protect grafts.

Weeks 1-2

Early Healing

Scabs fall off; transplanted hairs often shed (shock loss) before a new growth phase begins. Continue prescribed topical treatments or oral medications.

Months 3-6

Hair Growth Phase

New hair appears gradually; substantial cosmetic improvement is typically visible around 6 months and continues to mature up to 12-18 months.

Long-Term

Maintenance

Some patients require additional sessions to increase density. Medical therapies like finasteride and minoxidil are commonly used to preserve native hair.

Frequently Asked Questions — Nanjing Clinic

Everything you need to know about visiting our Nanjing hair transplant center

Where is the Barley Nanjing clinic located, and which subway station is nearest?
Our Nanjing clinic is located on Level 16, Tower 2, Deji Plaza (Phase II), 18 Zhongshan Road, Gulou District, directly above Xinjiekou Station. Take Metro Lines 1 or 2 to Xinjiekou Station, exit through Exit 14, and you'll walk directly into the Deji Plaza mall complex. The clinic is a 3-minute elevator ride from the mall entrance. Xinjiekou is the commercial heart of Nanjing, steps from the iconic Nanjing Library and only two stops from Nanjing University Gulou campus.
What are the opening hours for the Nanjing clinic? Do you open on weekends and public holidays?
Our Gulou District clinic is open Monday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, including most public holidays. Consultations can be booked between 9:30 AM and 5:30 PM. Surgical procedures are scheduled Tuesday through Saturday, generally starting at 8:30 AM for morning sessions and 1:00 PM for afternoon sessions. Sunday is reserved for post-op checkups and non-surgical treatments (PRP, scalp care, mesotherapy). We recommend booking at least 3-4 weeks in advance if you prefer a Friday or Saturday procedure slot.
How do I get to the Nanjing clinic from Lukou International Airport and Nanjing South Railway Station?
From Nanjing Lukou International Airport: Take Metro Line S1 (Airport Line) to Nanjing South Station, transfer to Line 1 heading toward Maigaoqiao, and ride 8 stops to Xinjiekou Station (about 70 minutes total, approximately $1). A taxi takes 45-60 minutes depending on Zhongshan Road traffic and costs roughly $17-$23. From Nanjing South Railway Station (high-speed rail hub for Shanghai, Hangzhou, Hefei, and Beijing routes): Take Metro Line 1 northbound for 8 stops directly to Xinjiekou — it's a 25-minute ride for $1. Taxi from South Station to Xinjiekou runs about $6-$9 in 20-35 minutes.
I'm traveling from Anhui or Jiangxi province. What's the recommended way to plan my Nanjing trip?
Patients from Hefei, Wuhu, Ma'anshan (Anhui) and Nanchang, Jiujiang, Ganzhou (Jiangxi) make up roughly 40% of our Nanjing clinic patient base. We strongly recommend arriving the evening before via high-speed rail (Hefei South to Nanjing South = 58 minutes; Nanchang West to Nanjing South = approx. 2.5 hours). Book a hotel within Xinjiekou or Fuzimiao (Confucius Temple) area — both are walkable or one subway stop from the clinic. Plan for: Day 0 (arrival + evening consultation if needed), Day 1 (surgery day, 4-8 hours), Day 2 (first shampoo + dressing check at clinic), and Day 3 (departure). Our concierge can help arrange discounted rates at Novotel Nanjing Central and Intercontinental Nanjing.
Does the Nanjing clinic have a lead surgeon with exclusive specialties? What's their background?
Yes, our Nanjing clinic is led by Dr. Wang Zhaowen, who holds a medical degree from Nanjing Medical University and completed a 2-year hair restoration fellowship in Seoul, South Korea. Dr. Wang has performed over 5,200 hair transplant procedures and is Barley's nationwide trainer for no-shave microneedle hairline design. He has a particular expertise in: (1) temple point and sideburn refinement for younger patients in the university and tech sectors, and (2) corrective/repair procedures for patients who had unsatisfactory work in other cities. He personally sees all Grade 5+ baldness cases and oversees every graft above 2,500 units.
Which hotels do you recommend near the Nanjing clinic for out-of-town patients?
We have negotiated rates with three hotels within 8 minutes' walking distance of our Deji Plaza clinic. Budget option: Orange Hotel Select (Xinjiekou) — $54-$69/night, modern rooms, 5-minute walk, includes breakfast. Mid-range: Novotel Nanjing Central — $83-$103/night, international standard, connected to Deji Plaza via an underground walkway, our most popular choice. Luxury: The Ritz-Carlton Nanjing on the upper floors of Deji Plaza Tower 1 — $229+/night, elevator directly to clinic floor. Patients staying a minimum of 3 nights also receive complimentary access to our VIP recovery lounge. We recommend booking through our patient concierge for guaranteed clinic-nearby availability, especially during college graduation season and the annual Plum Blossom Festival.
What visa and entry conveniences does Nanjing offer international patients? Is there 144-hour transit?
Nanjing Lukou International Airport participates in China's 144-Hour Visa-Free Transit policy for citizens of 53 countries including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, and all EU nations. To qualify, you must hold a valid passport, an onward ticket to a third country departing within 144 hours from any Chinese port of entry, and disembark at Lukou Airport. This is perfect for patients combining their procedure with a visit to Nanjing's historic sites (Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum at Purple Mountain, Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, and Fuzimiao). For patients needing more than 6 days, we provide formal medical invitation letters that can support a standard M-type (business) or L-type (tourist) visa application through your local Chinese consulate. Our concierge team handles all required clinic documentation.
