Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about hair transplant in Shijiazhuang

How much does hair transplant cost in Shijiazhuang?

Cost varies based on the number of grafts needed and technique used. On average, FUE costs approx USD 1.4-2.1 per graft, while our proprietary microneedle technique costs approx USD 2.1-2.9 per graft. A typical session ranges from approx USD 1,429-4,286.

How long does the procedure take?

Most procedures take 4-8 hours depending on the number of grafts. Smaller sessions (800-2000 grafts) may take 4-5 hours, while larger sessions (3000+ grafts) can take 6-8 hours or may be split across multiple days.

What is the recovery time?

Most patients can return to work within 3-5 days. Initial healing takes about 10-14 days. New hair growth typically begins around 3-4 months, with full results visible at 12-18 months post-procedure.

Is the result permanent?

Yes, transplanted hair is permanent because it comes from the donor area (back and sides of head) which is resistant to hair loss. However, native hair may continue to thin, so maintenance treatments may be recommended.

Do you offer consultations for international patients?

Yes, we offer online consultations for international patients. We can assess your hair loss, discuss treatment options, and provide a cost estimate before you travel to Shijiazhuang. English-speaking staff are available.

What techniques do you offer?

We offer FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction), FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation), and our proprietary microneedle transplant technique. During consultation, we'll recommend the best technique based on your specific needs and goals.
