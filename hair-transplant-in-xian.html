Why Choose Xi'an for Hair Transplant

The ancient Silk Road capital — now Northwest China's premier medical tourism hub on the Belt & Road route

Xi'an serves as the undisputed hair restoration hub for the entire Northwest China region — Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai, and Xinjiang provinces. As a starting point of the ancient Silk Road and now a key Belt & Road Initiative city, Xi'an has long been a crossroads for travelers, making it the natural choice for combining medical treatment with cultural tourism.

Medical and tourism synergy is Xi'an's unique advantage. Many international visitors — particularly the large numbers of Korean tourists who visit Xi'an annually for the Terracotta Warriors and Giant Wild Goose Pagoda — schedule their hair transplant procedures to coincide with their sightseeing itineraries, saving both time and travel costs.

The city's status as a university and research center ensures a deep talent pool of highly trained medical professionals. Xi'an is also significantly more affordable than Beijing or Shanghai, making it the cost-effective gateway for all five Northwestern provinces.

Common Hair Transplant Techniques

Advanced methods available at our Xi'an Gaoxin District clinic

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)

FUE removes individual follicular units from the donor area and implants them into the recipient area.

  • Minimal linear scarring
  • Faster healing time
  • Suitable for short hairstyles
  • Natural-looking results

FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation)

FUT removes a linear strip of scalp from the donor area, from which grafts are dissected.

  • High graft yield in single session
  • Cost-effective for large areas
  • Suitable for advanced baldness
  • Proven long-term results

Microneedle Transplant

Advanced technique using precision microneedle implant pens for superior results.

  • Higher precision placement
  • Better graft survival rate
  • Minimal discomfort
  • Barley's proprietary technology

Choosing the Right Clinic and Surgeon

Essential factors for Northwest China and Korean medical tourists selecting Xi'an providers

Credentials and Experience

Verify surgeon qualifications, board certifications, and years of focused hair-restoration experience. Confirm Northwest-typical hair texture expertise for Xinjiang/Hui/Mongolian patients.

Before-and-After Portfolios

Ask for unedited before-and-after photos of patients with similar ethnic hair types, including Korean, Uyghur, Tibetan, and Hui patients if applicable to your background.

Techniques and Team

Confirm whether the surgeon personally performs key steps (extraction, recipient site creation, implantation) and whether a trained team supports the operation.

Accreditation and Hygiene

Look for hospital or clinic accreditation, transparent infection-control protocols, and modern operating-room standards suitable for international patients.

Transparent Pricing

Ensure itemized estimates that clearly show what's included (consultation, surgery, anesthesia, follow-ups, medications). Ask about Korean-language coordination if needed.

Language & Travel Support

Korean-speaking staff, Belt & Road multilingual support, airport pickup, and hotel-tourism package coordination are must-haves for international visitors to Xi'an.

Frequently Asked Questions - Xi'an Clinic

Everything about hair transplant in Xi'an, the Northwest China and Belt & Road capital

Where is the Barley Xi'an clinic located?

Our Xi'an flagship clinic is located in the Gaoxin High-Tech District at Building C, International Commerce Center, 38 Gaoxin Road, Yanta District, Xi'an. This prestigious business district puts us 15 minutes by taxi from the Terracotta Warriors shuttle bus station and 8 minutes from the scenic Giant Wild Goose Pagoda. The modern facility occupies 2 full floors with 8 premium operating rooms, a VIP consultation suite, dedicated Korean-language reception desk, and a post-op recovery lounge with complimentary Chinese tea service. The Gaoxin Road Metro Station (Line 3, Exit A) is literally 2 minutes away on foot, making us incredibly accessible from all parts of the city.

What are the Xi'an clinic's business hours?

Our Xi'an Yanta Gaoxin clinic operates Monday through Sunday from 8:30 AM to 7:30 PM to accommodate both early-morning arrivals and evening consultations after sightseeing. Surgical procedures run Monday through Saturday 9:00 AM — 6:00 PM. For the convenience of Korean tourists who book 3-5 day Xi'an travel packages, we reserve 5 Saturday procedure slots per month specifically for international visitors — please request these at least 4 weeks in advance. Initial consultations in Korean, Japanese, and Russian are available by appointment. We also open on selected public holidays during peak tourism season (March-May and September-November) when visitor volumes to the Terracotta Warriors are highest.

How do I reach the clinic from Xi'an Xianyang International Airport or railway stations?

From Xi'an Xianyang International Airport (XIY): We strongly recommend the Airport Shuttle Bus Line 4 (Gaoxin Line), which takes you directly to the Gaoxin Intercontinental Hotel stop — a 5-minute walk from our clinic. Total journey is approximately 70 minutes for ~USD 7. Metro option: Airport Intercity Railway to North Railway Station, transfer to Line 2 to Xiaozhai, then Line 3 to Gaoxin Road (around 90 minutes total). Complimentary private Mercedes Viano pickup is provided for patients booking 3,000+ graft procedures or Korean premium package patients. From Xi'an North Railway Station (high-speed rail for Gansu/Ningxia/Xinjiang connections): Take Metro Line 2 directly to Xiaozhai Station, change to Line 3 for 2 stops west to Gaoxin Road Station (total 50 minutes, ~USD 1). From Xi'an Station (old town): Bus 608 directly to Gaoxin Road stop, or taxi for ~USD 6 in 30 minutes depending on traffic.

Which provinces and regions does the Xi'an clinic primarily serve?

As the designated Northwest China regional hub, our Xi'an clinic serves the full 5-province Northwestern administrative region: Shaanxi Province (all cities including Baoji, Xianyang, Yan'an, and Hanzhong), Gansu Province (Lanzhou, Tianshui, Dunhuang, Jiuquan), Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region (Yinchuan, Shizuishan, Wuzhong), Qinghai Province (Xining, Golmud, Yushu Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture), and Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region (Urumqi, Kashgar, Karamay, Turpan, and the entire Silk Road Economic Belt corridor). Additionally, we receive significant international patient traffic from South Korea (tourism + treatment packages), Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, and other Belt & Road Central Asian countries due to Xi'an's direct flight connections and visa-on-arrival policies for many nationalities. We maintain dedicated Russian- and Korean-speaking patient coordinators on staff full-time.

Who is the lead surgeon at the Xi'an clinic?

The Xi'an Gaoxin clinic's chief surgeon is Dr. Li Wenqiang, a graduate of Xi'an Jiaotong University Health Science Center (formerly Xi'an Medical University) with 16 years of exclusive hair restoration experience. Dr. Li completed a 2-year clinical fellowship at the Seoul National University Bundang Hospital's Hair Transplant Center, where he specialized in high-density microneedle transplantation techniques popular with Korean patients. He has performed over 5,800 FUE and microneedle procedures in his career, with particular expertise in multi-ethnic hair transplantation (serving many Uyghur, Hui, Korean, and Central Asian patients with varying curl patterns and skin types), hairline feminization surgery for the LGBTQ+ community, and revision procedures correcting poor work from less-experienced clinics in smaller NW cities. Dr. Li leads our quarterly Belt & Road Medical Hair Symposium and speaks basic conversational Korean for international patient consultations.

What food and sightseeing do you recommend combining with my Xi'an appointment?

Xi'an is China's historical treasure chest — perfect for combining treatment with once-in-a-lifetime cultural experiences. Food recommendations (all gentle enough for week 1+ recovery!): Xi'an Muslim Street (Huimin Jie) — 10 minutes from the clinic via Line 3. Must-tries: roujiamo (Chinese hamburger), liangpi cold noodles (no spice level request!), yangrou paomo mutton bread soup, and persimmon cakes. Our Korean patients also love the H-Mart-style Korean food court at CityOn Mall Gaoxin for familiar flavors. Sightseeing itinerary, recovery-ranked: Days 0-3 post-surgery = Big Wild Goose Pagoda North Plaza musical fountain show (spectacular at night, requires zero walking). Days 4-7 = Xi'an City Wall rent an electric golf cart for a leisurely lap (no biking!). Week 2 onward = full-day Terracotta Warriors & Horses Museum + Huaqing Hot Springs. Week 3+ = Mount Hua cable car day trip (avoid the hiking trails per clinic advice). We also recommend the Tang Dynasty Music & Dance Show dinner theater — a beloved cultural evening that requires no physical exertion. The clinic's Korean coordinator maintains pre-vetted partner tour agencies for Korean-language tours.
