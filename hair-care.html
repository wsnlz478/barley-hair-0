Immediate Aftercare (First 2 Weeks)

Critical steps to protect your new grafts during the most important recovery window

Day 1-3

Critical Protection Phase

The first 72 hours are the most crucial period for graft survival. Freshly implanted follicles have no blood supply yet and must anchor into your scalp. Any disturbance can dislodge them permanently.

  • Do not touch, scratch, or rub the transplanted area
  • Sleep with head elevated at 45 degrees to reduce swelling
  • Use only saline spray as directed by your surgeon
  • Avoid bending over, lifting, or any strenuous activity
  • No alcohol, smoking, or blood-thinning medications
Day 4-7

Gentle Washing Begins

You may begin gentle washing after 72 hours. Scabs will start forming around the grafts — these must fall off naturally. Never pick or scratch at them.

  • Wash with lukewarm water using a cup — never direct shower spray
  • Use only the provided sulfate-free shampoo, applied gently
  • Pat dry with a clean paper towel — never rub
  • Continue sleeping with head elevated
  • Light walking is fine; avoid sweating entirely
Week 2

Scab Resolution & Normalization

By the end of week two, scabs should have fallen off naturally. The transplanted area will look red but is healing well underneath. Grafts are now securely anchored.

  • Gradually return to normal washing with fingertip motions
  • Scabs should be completely gone — see your doctor if not
  • You may start using a hair dryer on cool setting at 15cm distance
  • Light exercise can resume — no contact sports or swimming
  • Continue avoiding direct sun exposure and tight headwear

Essential Care Practices

Follow these daily practices to maximize your transplant results

Washing Technique

Use lukewarm water and sulfate-free shampoo. Pour water gently — never use direct spray. Pat dry with soft towel, never rub. After 2 weeks, use light fingertip circular motions only.

Sleeping Position

Sleep on your back with head elevated at 45° for the first week. Use extra pillows or a wedge cushion. Avoid side or stomach sleeping to prevent friction on grafts.

Nutrition & Diet

Eat protein-rich foods: eggs, fish, lean meats, nuts, and leafy greens. Key nutrients include iron, zinc, biotin, and vitamins A, C, D, E. Stay hydrated — limit alcohol and processed foods.

Sun Protection

Wear a loose-fitting hat when outdoors for 4-6 weeks. After healing, apply mineral SPF 30+ to scalp. UV exposure can cause discoloration and compromise graft recovery.

Headwear Rules

No hats for the first 3-5 days. After that, only loose-fitting clean hats that don't touch grafts. Avoid tight caps, beanies, or helmets for at least 2-3 weeks.

Exercise Guidelines

Light walking is fine immediately. Avoid strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, and sweating for 2 weeks. Swimming: wait 4 weeks. Full routine: resume after 3-4 weeks.

Long-Term Hair Health Tips

Expert advice to keep your transplanted and existing hair healthy for life

1

Avoid Chemical Treatments

Wait at least 6 months before coloring, perming, or bleaching. These chemicals can irritate the scalp and damage newly established grafts. Choose ammonia-free formulas when you do.

2

Minimize Heat Styling

Use cool or low-heat settings only. Keep the dryer at least 15cm from your scalp. Air drying with a soft towel is the safest option during the first few months.

3

Protect Against DHT

For men, discuss DHT-blocking treatments with your doctor. Finasteride and natural DHT blockers can protect both transplanted and existing hair from hormonal thinning.

4

Maintain a Balanced Diet

Continue eating protein-rich foods with iron, zinc, and omega-3s. Limit processed foods, excessive sugar, and alcohol — all of which can weaken hair and slow growth.

5

Attend Follow-Up Appointments

Scheduled check-ups at 3, 6, and 12 months let our doctors track growth, catch issues early, and adjust your care plan. These are included in your treatment package.

6

Manage Stress Levels

High stress accelerates hair loss through telogen effluvium. Practice relaxation techniques, get adequate sleep, and consider scalp massage to improve circulation and reduce tension.

Common Questions About Hair Care

Expert answers for post-transplant and daily hair maintenance

What non-surgical hair care packages does Barley offer
We have six main non-surgical care lines: the Scalp Deep-Cleansing SPA for oily/dandruff-prone scalps, the Anti-Hair-Loss Maintenance program for early-stage thinning patients, the Women's Densifying Care package designed specifically for female pattern thinning, the LLLT Laser Growth System that combines red light with nutrient serums, the Post-Transplant Sensitive Care course for newly operated patients, and the Premium Age-Defying Scalp package with microneedling and peptide serums for mature clients. Each starts with a free trichoscopy scalp scan so we can match you to the right package instead of upselling blindly.
How often should I come in for hair care maintenance sessions
It depends on your scalp type and which program you're on, but the baseline recommendation is every 3 to 4 weeks for ongoing maintenance. For the intensive 6-week anti-hair-loss course, clients come in once every 7 days for the first 6 visits, then drop back to every 4 weeks for maintenance. Oily scalps with chronic dandruff usually do best every 10 days initially, then stretch to every 2-3 weeks once the issue is under control. Our trichologists build you a custom calendar during your first scan and you can book everything in advance through our app.
Can non-surgical hair care replace the need for a hair transplant
Honestly, sometimes yes and sometimes no — it completely depends on what stage of hair loss you're at. For Norwood 1-2 early thinning with mostly miniaturized vellus hairs still present, or for women with diffuse Ludwig pattern thinning, a consistent 6-12 month care program can absolutely thicken existing hair enough that a transplant is never needed. But once you're at Norwood 3+ with visible bald patches where the follicles have completely miniaturized away, non-surgical care can slow further loss and improve scalp health but can't regrow hair that's permanently gone — at that point a transplant is the only permanent solution. We'll be upfront about this during your scan instead of selling you futile packages.
Does oil-control and anti-dandruff care work in one session or require a course
You will absolutely notice a visible difference after just one deep-cleansing SPA session — your scalp will feel immediately cleaner, less itchy, and flaking is significantly reduced within 48 hours. But for chronic, long-standing dandruff or seborrheic dermatitis, one treatment doesn't reset the scalp microbiome permanently. We typically recommend a starter course of 4 sessions spaced 10 days apart to fully clear the underlying issue, then a maintenance session every 3-4 weeks to keep it from coming back. Roughly 70% of our clients can stop maintenance entirely after 6 months; the other 30% just do the occasional session when they feel a flare-up starting.
Does your hair care program work for female androgenetic alopecia
Yes — and female pattern thinning is actually one of our highest-success indications for non-surgical care. Women with Ludwig stage 1 or 2 diffuse thinning (especially those who want to avoid oral finasteride or minoxidil) respond extremely well to our women-specific program. The protocol combines LLLT laser sessions, topical herbal anti-androgen serums without systemic absorption, gentle microneedle dermarolling to boost product penetration, and custom nutrient ampoules. In our 2025 patient data, 83% of women with early Ludwig pattern saw measurable thickening of their part line by month 6, and roughly half avoided needing any surgical intervention entirely. Results do vary based on how long the thinning has been present.
Do you offer packages that include LLLT low-level laser light therapy
Absolutely — LLLT laser therapy is included in almost all our care packages as a foundational treatment. We use medical-grade 650nm red light laser helmets with 160+ diodes that deliver consistent photon energy to wake up dormant follicles and reduce scalp inflammation. Both our Anti-Hair-Loss Maintenance program and Women's Densifying Care package include a 20-minute LLLT session at every visit. For clients who want to continue therapy at home, we also offer discounted portable home-use laser caps that are calibrated to match our in-clinic protocols, sold separately or bundled with 10-session packages at a reduced rate.
What is the general price range for Barley's hair care packages
Our hair care pricing is significantly more affordable than equivalent aesthetic clinics in the US or UK. Single sessions start at roughly $53–$95 USD depending on which treatment you choose. The most popular 10-session bundled packages range from about $445 USD for the basic Scalp Cleansing SPA up to about $1,222 USD for the Premium LLLT Growth System, which works out to roughly $44–$122 USD per session when bought in bulk. We also offer interest-free payment plans split over 3 or 6 months for domestic clients, and international patients paying by card or wire get an additional 5% off prepaid packages. Exact pricing is provided after your free trichoscopy scan, since we tailor the exact protocol to your scalp needs.
