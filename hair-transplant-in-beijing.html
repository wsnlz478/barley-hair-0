Common Hair Transplant Techniques

Understanding the different methods available for hair restoration in Beijing

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)

FUE removes individual follicular units from the donor area and implants them into the recipient area.

  • Minimal linear scarring
  • Faster healing time
  • Suitable for short hairstyles
  • Natural-looking results

FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation)

FUT removes a linear strip of scalp from the donor area, from which grafts are dissected.

  • High graft yield in single session
  • Cost-effective for large areas
  • Suitable for advanced baldness
  • Proven long-term results

Microneedle Transplant

Advanced technique using precision microneedle implant pens for superior results.

  • Higher precision placement
  • Better graft survival rate
  • Minimal discomfort
  • Barley's proprietary technology

Choosing the Right Clinic and Surgeon in Beijing

Key factors to consider when selecting a hair transplant provider in Beijing

Credentials and Experience

Verify surgeon qualifications, board certifications, and years of focused hair-restoration experience. High-volume experience correlates with predictable graft survival and natural-looking hairlines.

Before-and-After Portfolios

Ask for unedited before-and-after photos of patients with similar hair loss patterns and ethnic hair types. Request contactable patient references when possible.

Techniques and Team

Confirm whether the surgeon personally performs key steps (extraction, recipient site creation, implantation) and whether a trained team supports the operation.

Accreditation and Hygiene

Look for hospital or clinic accreditation, transparent infection-control protocols, and modern operating-room standards.

Transparent Pricing

Ensure itemized estimates that clearly show what's included (consultation, surgery, anesthesia, follow-ups, medications). Avoid hidden fees.

Communication and Support

Choose a clinic with English-speaking staff or professional translation services. Clear communication is essential for your safety and satisfaction.

Recovery Timeline and Aftercare

What to expect during your hair transplant recovery journey in Beijing

Days 1-3

Immediate Post-Op

Scabbing, mild swelling, and tenderness are common. Follow clinic guidance on cleaning, sleeping position, and activity restrictions to protect grafts.

Weeks 1-2

Early Healing

Scabs fall off; transplanted hairs often shed (shock loss) before a new growth phase begins. Continue prescribed topical treatments or oral medications.

Months 3-6

Hair Growth Phase

New hair appears gradually; substantial cosmetic improvement is typically visible around 6 months and continues to mature up to 12-18 months.

Long-Term

Maintenance

Some patients require additional sessions to increase density. Medical therapies like finasteride and minoxidil are commonly used to preserve native hair.

Beijing Clinic FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our Beijing headquarters clinic location, travel, and services

Where is your Beijing clinic located, and which subway station is closest?

Our Barley Beijing headquarters clinic is located at Tower B, SOHO Sanlitun, 19 Gongti North Road, Chaoyang District. The closest subway station is Tuanjiehu Station on Line 10 (Exit D), approximately a 6-minute walk. You can also reach us from Dongzhimen Station via a short taxi ride (about 12 minutes). The clinic is surrounded by premium hotels, restaurants, and the famous Sanlitun shopping district.

What are the opening hours for the Beijing clinic?

Our Beijing headquarters clinic operates Monday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, including most national holidays. Surgical procedures are typically scheduled between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM, while consultations and follow-ups can be arranged through 7:00 PM. We recommend booking your preferred time slot at least 3 days in advance. International patients may request Saturday appointments to accommodate weekend travel.

How do I travel from Beijing Capital Airport or Daxing Airport to the clinic?

From Capital Airport Terminal 3: Take the Airport Express train to Dongzhimen Station (approximately 30 minutes), then take a 12-minute taxi to Sanlitun SOHO. From Daxing International Airport: Take the Daxing Airport Express to Caoqiao, transfer to Line 10 heading to Jiaomen West, and ride 14 stops to Tuanjiehu. The full journey takes roughly 75 minutes. We also offer complimentary private airport transfer service for international patients booking 2,000+ graft packages — please request this when scheduling.

What travel advice do you give patients from North China, Northeast China, or Inner Mongolia?

Patients from Hebei, Shanxi, Shandong, Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang, and Inner Mongolia typically arrive via Beijing South Station or Beijing West Station on the high-speed rail network. From Beijing South, take Metro Line 4 to Haidianhuangzhuang, transfer to Line 10, and exit at Tuanjiehu (total ~35 minutes). We recommend staying at least 3 nights for a standard procedure: arrival day for consultation and blood tests, surgery day, and a follow-up wash the day after. Winter patients should bring a warm loose hat for the first week outdoors.

What surgical team qualifications and quality standards does Barley follow for hair restoration procedures?

Barley maintains strict qualification standards across all our clinic locations. Every lead surgeon must hold a valid Chinese medical license with at least 8 years of specialized hair restoration experience, a minimum track record of 3,000 completed transplant procedures, and documented training in our proprietary microneedle implantation system. Each surgical team includes a licensed extraction surgeon, 2-4 trained graft placement technicians, and a dedicated circulating sterile assistant. Our internal audit committee reviews every surgeon's survival rate data, patient satisfaction scores, and complication reports quarterly. Before your procedure, we are happy to share the surgical team's collective experience profile, average graft survival metrics, and anonymized before/after portfolio samples from cases similar to your planned session.

Which hotels do you recommend near the Beijing clinic?

For international patients we suggest three tiers near Wangfujing and Sanlitun: Luxury option — Grand Hyatt Beijing on Wangfujing Street (10-minute taxi ride, ~$220/night). Mid-range — Holiday Inn Express Beijing Sanlitun (4-minute walk, ~$95/night). Budget-friendly — Home Inn Plus Dongzhimen (8-minute walk, ~$53/night). All are within walking distance to restaurants, pharmacies, and shopping. Patients who book through our concierge receive a negotiated 15-20% discount at partner hotels.

Is there a visa-free transit policy for foreigners visiting Beijing?

Yes — Beijing Capital Airport and Daxing Airport both participate in China's 144-hour visa-free transit policy for passport holders from 53 eligible countries including the USA, UK, EU nations, Canada, Australia, Japan, and South Korea. You can stay up to 6 full days in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region without a traditional visa as long as you hold confirmed onward tickets to a third country. The clinic can provide an official invitation letter to support your visa-on-arrival or standard tourist visa (L-visa) application if needed.
