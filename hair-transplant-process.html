The Complete Hair Transplant Journey

Undergoing a hair transplant is a significant decision, and understanding every step of the process helps reduce anxiety and set realistic expectations. At Barley Hospital, we guide you through each phase with complete transparency - from your very first consultation to your final results twelve to fifteen months later.

Whether you are travelling from overseas or coming from within China, our team ensures a smooth and well-organised experience. This guide walks you through every stage so you know precisely what to expect, how to prepare, and how to care for yourself during recovery.

Phase 1: Before the Procedure

Preparation is the foundation of successful results

1

Initial Consultation

Thorough evaluation of scalp condition, donor area density, and hair characteristics. Digital imaging simulates expected results. Customised treatment plan with transparent pricing.

2

Pre-Operative Preparation

Two weeks before: stop smoking and reduce alcohol. One week before: discontinue blood-thinning medications. Three days before: avoid caffeine and alcohol. Day before: rest and wash hair.

3

Travel and Arrival

For international patients: airport pickup, hotel booking, and English-speaking coordinator provided. Final consultation, blood tests if needed, and pre-surgery photographs taken.

Phase 2: Surgery Day

Your comfortable journey through the procedure

1

Morning: Preparation

Arrive after light breakfast. Team reviews your plan. Surgeon marks new hairline with surgical pen. You approve the design before work begins.

2

Local Anaesthesia

Anaesthesia administered to donor and recipient areas. Brief pinching sensations (30-60 seconds). Once numb, you feel no pain.

3

Midday: Graft Extraction

Precision micro-punch tools extract individual follicular units. Grafts placed in preservation solution and sorted under microscopes. Takes 2-4 hours.

4

Recipient Site Creation

Surgeon creates thousands of tiny channels at precise angles and directions to mimic natural hair growth patterns.

5

Afternoon: Implantation

Grafts carefully implanted using forceps or Choi pens. Each placed at correct angle, depth, and direction. Full procedure takes 4-8 hours.

6

Comfort & Care

Listen to music, watch movies, or nap during procedure. Regular breaks for lunch and refreshments. Detailed aftercare instructions provided.

Phase 3: Immediate Recovery

What to expect in the first week after surgery

1

Day of Surgery

Head home with detailed aftercare instructions. Sleep elevated at 45 degrees. Minor oozing of clear fluid is normal. Take prescribed medications as directed. Do not touch or scratch the transplanted area.

2

Days 1-3

Forehead swelling may peak. Begin gentle saline spray as instructed. Some tightness in the donor area is normal. Continue medications. Avoid bending over or lifting heavy objects. Light walking encouraged.

3

Days 4-7

Begin gentle washing with prescribed shampoo. Small scabs begin forming. Swelling subsides. Most patients return to desk work. Avoid direct sun exposure and strenuous activity.

Phase 4: Healing and Growth

The patience phase - results are developing beneath the surface

Weeks 2-4

Scabs Fall Off & Shock Loss

Most scabs naturally fall off. Transplanted area may appear red and pink. Transplanted hairs begin to shed (shock loss) - this is completely normal. Follicles remain alive beneath the scalp, entering a resting phase.

Months 2-3

Resting Phase

Scalp appears mostly normal. Follicles rest beneath the skin, preparing for new growth. Slight redness continues to fade. Resume most normal activities including exercise.

Months 3-4

First Signs of Growth

New hair begins to emerge. Initial growth may appear thin and light in colour. Early signs that follicles have successfully taken root. Growth is sparse but gradually increases.

Months 5-6

Visible Thickening

New hair becomes noticeably thicker and darker. Real improvement becomes visible. Density increases significantly. Many patients feel their confidence returning.

Months 7-9

Significant Results

Transformation becomes truly visible. Hair density increases substantially. New hair blends seamlessly with existing hair. Hairline takes on natural, mature appearance.

Months 10-12

Near-Final Results

Results approaching final state. Hair thickness, density, and texture continue to mature. Transplanted area looks completely natural. Style, cut, and treat like existing hair.

Months 12-15

Full Mature Results

Hair transplant reaches final, mature state. Results are permanent - transplanted hair continues growing for life. You now have a full, natural-looking head of hair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical details about the day, the clinic routine, and what you can and cannot do during surgery

Can I eat normally on surgery day, or do I have to fast
Please eat a full, normal breakfast before arriving - you do not need to fast since we use only local anaesthesia, not general. Avoid heavy caffeine or excessive liquid if you tend to need the toilet frequently. We provide bottled water, herbal tea, and a light lunch or snacks partway through the procedure. Tell the coordinator if you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions beforehand and we will accommodate them.
Am I allowed to drive myself home after the procedure
No, we strongly recommend you do not drive yourself. Even though local anaesthesia does not affect your judgement, you will be wearing a bandage, your neck may be stiff from the extraction position, and the anti-inflammatory medication can make you drowsy. If you are local, please arrange a taxi, a ride from a friend, or a pickup through the clinic. International patients are always transferred by our driver back to the hotel. You can safely drive again from day 3 onward once you are off any prescription pain medication.
Do I have to lie face down for the entire graft extraction
You will be face down for most of the donor extraction phase, which typically lasts 2.5 to 4 hours depending on graft count, but the table has a face cradle with breathing space and chest padding. We also offer a reclined side position option for patients who dislike being prone. The team will proactively suggest a break roughly every 60 to 75 minutes so you can stretch your legs, reposition, or use the restroom. If you have back problems, mention it in advance and we will add extra lumbar support.
Can I use my phone, watch movies, or read during implantation
Yes, absolutely - during implantation you are usually in a reclined or sitting position with your head supported, so watching Netflix or YouTube, listening to music or podcasts, or even reading on a tablet is fully possible. We provide a wall-mounted phone holder and Wi-Fi password on arrival. The only restriction is during the hairline design and anaesthesia phases when you need to keep still and follow directions. Scrolling is fine, but avoid loud ringtones that distract the team.
What if I need to go to the bathroom in the middle of the procedure
Just say the word and we will pause the current phase within 30 seconds. A nurse will help you sit up slowly, remove the sterile drapes around your body, and walk with you to the private patient restroom inside the surgery suite. We will never rush you or make you feel like you are being a burden. Most patients ask for two to three breaks across a full 6 to 7 hour procedure, which is expected and fully built into the schedule.
Will my English-speaking translator be in the operating room the entire time
Yes. Your dedicated translator stays in the room for the full procedure, never in a separate booth. They translate every comment the surgeon makes about hairline angle, graft direction, or zone density so you can follow along in real time. They also handle any requests you have mid-surgery - from adjusting music volume to asking for water or an extra blanket. All our translators are clinic employees with medical terminology training, not third-party agency staff.
Can my spouse, family member, or friend come into the OR with me
Family members cannot enter the sterile operating theatre itself for infection control reasons, but we have three comfortable options instead. They can wait in the private VIP lounge directly outside the OR door with coffee, snacks, and Wi-Fi, or step in for a quick 2-minute photo after the hairline design phase if you want their input on the shape. After the procedure is complete, they are welcome inside to see the final result and hear the aftercare instructions together with you.
