Hair Transplant in Hefei

Your complete guide to hair restoration in Hefei

Hefei, the capital of Anhui Province and a vibrant sub-center of the Yangtze River Delta, is a fast-growing science and education hub home to USTC (University of Science and Technology of China) and HFUT (Hefei University of Technology), as well as EV giants NIO and BYD. Barley Hair Transplant Hospital's Hefei clinic brings world-class hair restoration to the region, serving patients from central Anhui, northern Jiangsu, and northern Jiangxi.

Why choose Hefei for hair transplant? As one of China's fastest-developing cities, Hefei offers modern medical infrastructure combined with competitive pricing. Our clinic, conveniently located for patients traveling from Baohe District and surrounding areas, maintains the same high standards as all Barley locations while providing a comfortable, patient-focused environment.

Our Hefei clinic offers comprehensive hair restoration services including FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction), FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation), and our proprietary microneedle transplant technique. Whether you're dealing with hairline recession, thinning hair, or complete baldness, our experienced team provides personalized solutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about hair transplant in Hefei

Where is the Barley Hefei clinic located?

Our Hefei clinic is conveniently located in Shushan District near Swan Lake (Tian'e Hu), close to the Hefei Municipal Government complex. The exact address is 88 Tian'e Hu Avenue, Shushan District, Hefei. The area is well-connected with ample parking and easy access from all parts of the city, including Baohe District and the Hefei Hi-Tech Zone where USTC, NIO, and BYD facilities are located.

What are the clinic opening hours?

The Barley Hefei clinic is open Monday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We operate year-round including most public holidays. Consultation appointments are available during all business hours, while surgeries are scheduled Tuesday through Saturday to accommodate our medical team's schedule. We recommend booking 2-3 weeks in advance for weekend slots, which are popular with busy professionals from NIO, BYD, and the university districts.

How do I get to the clinic from Xinqiao Airport or Hefei South Station?

From Hefei Xinqiao International Airport: Take Airport Bus Line 4 (Xinqiao Airport to Hefei South Station direction), alight at the Swan Lake stop, then transfer to Metro Line 2 (towards Nangang direction) for 2 stops to Tian'e Hu Station. Exit A is 5 minutes' walk to our clinic. From Hefei South Railway Station: Take Metro Line 2 directly to Tian'e Hu Station (6 stops, approx. 15 minutes). We also offer complimentary private car pickup for international patients and those booking 2000+ graft procedures.

Is Hefei a good choice for patients coming from surrounding regions?

Absolutely. Hefei is the natural medical hub for central Anhui (Bengbu, Huainan, Anqing), northern Jiangsu (Xuzhou, Lianyungang, Suqian), and northern Jiangxi (Jiujiang, Shangrao, Jingdezhen). The high-speed rail network means most patients in these areas can reach Hefei South Station in 1-2.5 hours. Many patients combine their procedure with a short stay near Swan Lake or Baohe Park. We offer regional patient packages including hotel recommendations and follow-up consultations via video for those living further away.

What surgical team qualifications and quality standards does Barley Hefei follow?

Barley Microneedle Hair Transplant Hospital maintains strict medical team qualification standards across all clinics. Our Hefei clinic's chief surgical team all hold valid Chinese medical practitioner licenses with 12+ years of specialized hair restoration experience, trained in microneedle transplant techniques at leading Seoul medical centers. The team is particularly known for expertise in high-density hairline design for young professionals from the tech and EV sectors, as well as eyebrow and beard transplants with exceptional naturalness. All chief surgeons personally handle consultation and surgical planning for every patient, with documented track records of 6,000+ successful procedures per lead team member. Each clinic operates under hospital-level operating licenses, with sterile dedicated ORs, mandatory 24-month graft survival tracking, and transparent written itemized quotes.

What are recommended hotels near the Hefei clinic?

For mid-range options with excellent clinic proximity: 1) Holiday Inn Express Hefei Swan Lake (8-minute walk, 4-star, approx. $54 USD/night) — the most popular with our patients, includes breakfast. 2) Hilton Garden Inn Hefei Shushan (5-minute drive, approx. $64 USD/night) with airport shuttle. For premium stays: InterContinental Hefei Swan Lake (12-minute walk, 5-star, approx. $121 USD/night). We can arrange special patient rates at all three — just mention you're a Barley patient when booking. All hotels are smoke-free and offer quiet rooms ideal for post-op rest.

What local food and attractions can I enjoy during recovery in Hefei?

Hefei offers delicious local cuisine perfect for gentle post-op eating: try Luzhou Roast Duck at the iconic Tongqinglou Restaurant near Baohe Park (a 15-minute taxi ride), or Wushan Tribute Goose braised with soy sauce at family-run spots in Shushan. For a relaxing afternoon stroll during your first 3 days of recovery, walk the Swan Lake waterfront promenade right behind the clinic, or visit the picturesque Baohe Park scenic area with its lotus ponds and historic pagoda. If you extend your stay by 2-3 days after the initial rest period, the Anhui Museum and USTC's beautiful East Campus are lovely cultural stops. Avoid strenuous activities like hiking Dabie Mountains during your first two weeks.
