Review Transparency FAQ

Honest answers about the reviews published on this page

Are the reviews on this page from actual patients who received treatment at your clinics
Yes. Every review published on this page has been submitted by a verified patient who underwent a procedure at one of our Barley clinics. We cross-reference each submission against our internal patient database, surgical records, and follow-up photo history before publishing. No paid reviewers, no influencers, no fictional accounts are ever featured. You can trust that every experience described on this page is the genuine experience of a real patient.
Do you publish negative reviews and critical feedback, or only the positive ones
We publish every verified review we receive, including those expressing criticism or disappointment, provided they meet our basic content guidelines (no hate speech, no personally identifying staff members, no false claims). Patients who submit a verified review cannot remove it or edit it after publication. As you can see on this page, several 4.5-star and 4.8-star reviews reference specific criticisms alongside the praise — this is because we do not sanitize or filter feedback based on rating.
Can I contact the authors of these reviews to ask them questions about their experience
For patient privacy reasons, we cannot put prospective patients in direct contact with past patients without explicit written permission from both parties. That said, many published reviewers have opted into our patient-to-patient introduction program and have indicated a willingness to answer questions from people considering treatment. If you would like us to attempt an introduction, mention this during your free consultation and we will facilitate the connection on a case-by-case basis.
Have you ever paid patients for writing reviews or offered discounts in exchange for positive feedback
Absolutely not. Purchased, incentivized, or discounted reviews are forbidden by our internal policies. Patients submit reviews voluntarily through our post-operative follow-up portal, typically after their 6-month progress photo check-in. The submission form is unbranded and patients are not prompted, reminded, or compensated in any way for leaving feedback. This policy has been in place for over a decade and applies company-wide.
Why do some reviews mention they were posted from Google or Trustpilot while others are site submissions
We collect patient feedback through three channels simultaneously — our internal post-op portal, our Google Business profiles across all 31 locations, and our verified Trustpilot business page. Reviews marked as "Google" were originally submitted to Google Maps, republished here with the patient's permission and linked back to the original Google posting so you can cross-reference. Reviews without a platform tag were submitted directly through our website. All are treated equally in terms of verification and publication standards.
How recent are the reviews shown on this page
The reviews on this page are continuously updated. The featured reviews grid currently showcases submissions from the last 12 months, all from patients who have reached at least their 6-month follow-up milestone so their review reflects actual growth rather than just the surgical experience. We rotate in newer reviews quarterly so visitors always see feedback reflecting our current surgical protocols and current generation of instruments.
Is there a way to read more reviews before making my decision
Certainly. During your free video consultation, your coordinator can share additional unedited patient reviews organized by procedure type, nationality, and graft count. You can also find our complete unfiltered Google review feed by searching for "Barley Hair Transplant" on Google Maps (filter the results by your home country or specific service if desired). Combined, these sources represent over 8,000 published patient experiences.
