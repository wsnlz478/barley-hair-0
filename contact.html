Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about reaching us, booking your visit, and what to expect from first contact through procedure day

How do I book a free consultation as an international patient?
You can reach our international patient team through three channels: WhatsApp at +86 13730143529, WeChat at ID 15810155700, or email at hairtransplant@qq.com. WhatsApp tends to get the fastest response, typically within 2-4 hours during our business hours. Simply send your name, a brief note about your hair concerns, and your preferred timing. We'll confirm your consultation slot and send a short prep checklist with photo guidelines so we can give you the most accurate initial assessment.
Do I need to travel to China for the initial consultation or can it be done remotely?
We encourage all international patients to start with a remote consultation — there's no need to travel for the first step. A typical remote consultation includes a review of the scalp photos you submit, a 20-30 minute Zoom or WeChat video call with one of our senior surgeons, a written graft estimate and treatment recommendation, and a transparent cost breakdown. An in-person visit is only required on the actual day of the procedure itself, plus a day-one checkup. We've designed this process specifically to save our international patients time and travel cost.
What are the English-speaking support hours and how fast are replies?
Our international patient desk operates from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM China Standard Time (UTC+8), seven days a week. During these hours, WhatsApp and WeChat inquiries typically receive a first response within 1-3 hours, and emails within 2-4 hours. Messages sent outside business hours are answered in the order they were received, starting first thing the next morning. We maintain rotating weekend coverage so there's never a multi-day gap between your message and a real reply from a named coordinator you can build a relationship with.
What documents or photos should I prepare before my first message?
For the most useful initial assessment, please send five clear photos: your hairline from the front, the top of your scalp looking down, the back of your donor area, and both side profiles. Natural indoor lighting without filters works best. If you have photos showing your hair density from 3-5 years ago, those help us understand your baseline. It's also helpful to note any medications you currently take, prior hair procedures you've had, and any chronic medical conditions. None of these are mandatory to start the conversation — you can always send them later once we're in touch.
Will I be assigned a dedicated coordinator or will I speak to different people each time?
You'll have one primary international patient coordinator assigned to you from your very first message and that same person stays with you through every step: consultation, booking, travel prep, procedure-day logistics, and post-op check-ins. Our coordinators carry individual caseload caps specifically so they can build real relationships with each patient. This means you never have to repeat your medical history, re-explain travel constraints, or re-send photos to a new stranger. You'll also have their personal WhatsApp for any quick questions that come up after hours.
What payment methods do you accept and when is payment due?
To secure a confirmed surgery date we ask for a modest deposit, payable by Visa, MasterCard, international bank transfer, or major cryptocurrency wallets. The remaining balance is due on the morning of your procedure, again via card or bank transfer. We can also arrange installment plans for qualifying patients with verifiable employment — typically a 50% deposit and the balance split across 3-4 monthly installments post-procedure. Full pricing in your preferred currency, with any wire or card-processing fees spelled out, is provided in writing before you commit to anything.
Can a family member or friend accompany me, and how does that affect logistics?
Absolutely, and many of our patients do travel with a spouse, partner, or friend. Just mention it to your coordinator during booking and we'll adjust the hotel recommendation to a twin or double room, confirm the airport pickup vehicle size, and make sure the clinic waiting area is set up to accommodate your companion on procedure day. There's no extra fee from us for any of this. Your companion is welcome in the consultation room, during pre-op discussion, and in the recovery lounge afterward — we want whoever's supporting you to feel fully informed and comfortable as well.
What happens after I go home — how does the follow-up process work?
We schedule structured remote check-ins at day 1, day 3, day 7, 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 9 months, and 12 months post-procedure. Each check-in includes a short list of specific photos we'd like you to take and a simple message template you can reply to on WhatsApp. Our surgical team reviews your progress photos at each milestone and your coordinator calls or messages you back with feedback, any care adjustments, and answers to any new questions. You can also message your coordinator anytime between scheduled check-ins — there's no waiting for an appointment slot just to ask a quick question about shampooing, sun exposure, or anything else.
