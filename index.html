Hair Transplant FAQ

Common questions about the procedure, recovery, and results

What does the hair transplant process look like from start to finish
It starts with a free consultation where we analyze your scalp and discuss your goals. Next, we create a personalized treatment plan with a graft estimate and transparent pricing. On procedure day, you'll receive local anesthesia, follicles are extracted from your donor area, and then carefully implanted into the recipient zones using our microneedle pen. After a brief observation period, you can rest at your hotel. We'll see you the next day for a post-op check and gentle shampoo. The whole process is straightforward and our team guides you through every step.
How long does the actual procedure take
This depends on how many grafts you need. A typical hairline restoration (1,500-2,000 grafts) takes about 4-6 hours. Larger sessions covering both hairline and crown (3,000+ grafts) may run 6-8 hours. Don't worry though — you'll be awake but comfortably numb the entire time. Most patients watch movies, listen to podcasts, or even nap during the procedure. We schedule regular breaks for snacks, drinks, and stretching so you never feel rushed or uncomfortable.
Is the procedure painful? What about recovery discomfort
The procedure itself is virtually painless thanks to modern local anesthesia. The only discomfort you might feel is a few quick pinches during the numbing injections at the start — similar to getting dental work. After the anesthetic kicks in, you won't feel anything. For the first 1-2 days post-op, most patients describe mild soreness or tightness in the donor area, which is easily managed with over-the-counter painkillers. Any tenderness usually fades completely by day 3 or 4.
When will I start seeing real results from the transplant
Hair growth happens in phases, so patience is key. The transplanted hairs usually shed within the first 2-4 weeks — this is completely normal and expected. Around month 3, new hairs begin to sprout from the implanted follicles. By month 6, you'll see roughly 60-70% of the final growth and density. The full, mature results are visible at 12 months. We schedule milestone check-ins at each stage so you always know what's happening and what to expect next.
Are the results permanent, or will I lose the transplanted hair later
The transplanted hair is permanent. We extract follicles from the back and sides of your scalp — the "safe donor zone" where hair is genetically resistant to DHT, the hormone that causes pattern baldness. Once these DHT-resistant follicles are implanted, they keep their genetic properties and continue growing normally for the rest of your life. That said, your original non-transplanted hair may still thin over time, which is why we often recommend combining the procedure with preventive treatments like minoxidil or finasteride.
Do I need to come back for follow-up appointments after surgery
Yes, follow-up is an important part of getting great results. We typically see you the day after your procedure for a check-up and first professional shampoo. Then at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months. International patients can do these follow-ups via video call and photo submissions — we'll send clear instructions for how to take progress photos. Even after the 12-month mark, our team is always available if you have questions or want to check in.
How soon can I go back to work and normal daily activities
Most patients feel comfortable returning to desk jobs and light activities within 2-3 days. If you work from home, you could even be back at your laptop the following day — just keep the transplanted area protected and avoid leaning your head back against chairs. Strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, and swimming should wait 2-3 weeks. Sun exposure to the scalp needs to be limited for the first month, so wear a loose hat if you'll be outside. We give you a detailed timeline for every activity.
What if I don't know how many grafts I need
That's what the consultation is for! You don't need to figure this out on your own. Send us photos of your scalp from the front, top, sides, and back, and our surgeon will assess your hair loss stage, donor density, and what's needed to reach your goals. During the consultation, they'll explain exactly how many grafts they recommend and why, show you similar cases, and give you a precise quote. The whole point is to remove the guesswork so you know exactly what to expect.
