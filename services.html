Common Questions About Our Procedures

Discover answers to frequently asked questions by our patients

What hair restoration services does Barley offer overall
We offer nine core services: hairline transplantation, hair thickening for diffuse or crown thinning, complete baldness restoration, artistic eyebrow transplant, beard and mustache transplant, sideburns transplant, body hair transplant for chest or other regions, non-surgical hair loss treatment including PRP and medication, and ongoing hair care maintenance programs. Each service uses our patented microneedle implantation system.
Who is a suitable candidate for a hairline transplant versus hair thickening
Hairline transplantation is designed for patients whose frontal hairline has receded or who want a lower, more defined hairline shape. Hair thickening is for patients whose existing hair is diffusely thin or who have a see-through crown but still have native hair throughout. Many patients combine both services in a single session for a complete transformation. Your surgeon will recommend the best combination during your consultation.
How do eyebrow and beard transplants differ from regular scalp procedures
Facial hair procedures require much finer artistic control. Eyebrows use single-hair follicles placed at very specific feathering angles to mimic natural brow growth, and each brow arch is drawn freehand before surgery. Beard transplants match the coarser texture and downward sweeping direction of jawline and cheek hair. The donor hair selection also differs: eyebrow grafts come from the finer nape area, while beard grafts are selected from coarser occipital donor zones.
Can I combine multiple services in one surgical visit
Yes. Many international patients combine two or more services to maximize their trip. Popular combinations include hairline transplant plus crown thickening, hairline plus eyebrow transplant for women, and beard transplant plus sideburns transplant. The maximum combined graft count depends on your donor supply, but our team commonly handles 3,000-4,000 combined grafts in a single day. We'll confirm safe limits during your pre-op assessment.
What preparations are required before arriving for any service
Two weeks before your procedure, stop blood thinners such as aspirin and ibuprofen, avoid alcohol and smoking, and pause supplements like fish oil and vitamin E. Wash your hair thoroughly the morning of surgery and wear a buttoned or zippered shirt so nothing needs to be pulled over your head afterward. International patients should arrive two days early for blood work and a final in-clinic design session.
What does the post-procedure care package include
Every patient receives a printed aftercare booklet, clinic-formulated gentle shampoo and antiseptic spray, a soft protective pillow for sleeping, a sun hat, scalp moisturizer, and scheduled video follow-ups at one week, one month, three months, six months, and twelve months. International patients also receive a dedicated WhatsApp contact for any questions 24/7 during their recovery, plus complimentary PRP sessions if they return for follow-up visits.
Is the non-surgical hair loss treatment effective on its own
For early-stage thinning and patients under 30, the medical treatment program can halt loss and thicken existing hair shafts significantly without surgery. It combines prescription-grade topical finasteride or minoxidil, monthly in-clinic PRP injections, and a custom scalp wellness protocol. For advanced Norwood stages, we typically recommend combining medical treatment with transplantation so the transplanted grafts have the best possible scalp environment.
How should I decide between a body hair transplant and scalp donor hair
Scalp donor hair from the back of the head is always preferred because its longevity and thickness match the top of the scalp perfectly. Body hair (chest, beard, or nape) is used only when scalp donor supply is exhausted due to previous surgeries or very advanced baldness. Body hair can cover larger bald areas but may have slightly different texture, so your surgeon will plan blending zones carefully during the pre-op design.
